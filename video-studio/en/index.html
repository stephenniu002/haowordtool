About the video studio

What can I use for free?

Split scripts into scenes, edit headings, narration and timing, preview frames and export JSON or HyperFrames HTML projects. These tools work without an account.

How do I create a narrated video with captions?

Once production services are enabled, subscribers can generate ElevenLabs narration, render MP4 videos with HyperFrames or Remotion and download synced SRT captions. Unavailable services are clearly marked.

What does the subscription cost?

The monthly plan costs 30 USDT for 30 days. Renewal is manual, with no automatic charge. Alipay and WeChat amounts in CNY are shown at checkout.

Data & service information

Script splitting and previews run on your device; refreshing clears unsaved drafts. AI briefs go to xAI and narration scripts to ElevenLabs. Accounts, orders, videos and captions are stored by our production service. Payment providers process payments; we do not store bank card details. Use only content and voices you have rights to use. Captions / Mirage API access is pending; current captions use voiceover timestamps. Contact support to delete your account or work.
