Words Ending in ING

The ending -ing commonly forms present participles and gerunds in English. It also appears in nouns such as building and morning. Recognizing the base word makes both spelling and word-game searches easier.

Main spelling patterns

  • Most verbs simply add -ing: read → reading.
  • A final silent e is usually dropped: write → writing.
  • A short stressed vowel followed by one consonant may double that consonant: sit → sitting.
  • Words ending in ie usually change it to y: tie → tying.

These are practical patterns, not a complete grammar reference. Proper nouns, borrowed words and regional spelling can create exceptions.
