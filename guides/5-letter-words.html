How to narrow the list

  1. Place confirmed letters in their known positions.
  2. Keep present letters whose positions are unknown.
  3. Exclude letters already ruled out.
  4. Prefer common vowel and consonant combinations when several answers remain.

Choose informative letters

Words containing several distinct, common letters reveal more information than guesses with repeated letters. Depending on the puzzle, words such as crane, stare and point can test a useful mix of vowels and consonants. There is no universal best choice because every clue changes the remaining candidates.
