FAQ

Common questions

Does this guarantee Etsy ranking or sales?

No. The system helps sellers create clearer and more search-aware listing drafts, but it does not guarantee ranking, traffic, marketplace approval, or sales.

What AI tools does it work with?

It is designed for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another capable AI assistant. The prompts are written as reusable workflows rather than tool-specific shortcuts.

Is this only for Etsy?

The workflow is optimized for Etsy digital product listings, especially titles, tags, descriptions, delivery notes, and buyer messages. Many pieces can also be adapted for Shopify or other digital product stores.

Can I resell the raw files?

No. The product is for your own shop or business workflow. Reselling, redistributing, or publishing the raw files as a competing prompt pack is not included.
