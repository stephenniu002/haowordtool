A few things to know

How do I register?

Select “Create account with email”, enter your email on the Shopify page and complete verification. First-time customers use the same secure flow as returning customers.

What details do I need when I shop?

Use an email address you can access, such as Gmail. During Shopify checkout, enter the recipient’s name and full delivery address, including the postal code where required. Complete any requested contact details, such as a phone number. Review shipping options and the final total before paying.

Where is my email verification code?

Check the email address you entered, including your spam folder. Request another code on the Shopify sign-in page if needed.

Why can’t I see an order?

Sign in with the email address used for that purchase. Only orders placed with our store appear here. Purchases made through external AI providers or affiliate retailers are managed by those sellers.

Can I sign in with a phone number?

Email verification is the supported sign-in method linked here. Phone-only sign-in is not currently available on this website.
