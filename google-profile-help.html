Is Your Google Business Profile Costing You Calls?

When a customer searches for your kind of business, your Google profile is often the first thing they see — before your website, before your socials. A neglected profile quietly hands those customers to competitors.

The three most common problems we see

  • Missing or outdated photos. Profiles with few or old photos look inactive. Customers pick the business they can see.
  • Rating gaps. If your profile shows a lower rating than the same business gets on other review sites, Google visitors assume the worst number is the true one.
  • Unanswered reviews. A negative review with no response reads as "they don't care." A calm, professional reply can do more than the review itself hurt.

What a profile optimization service does

It isn't magic, and it isn't fake reviews. A legitimate optimization service does the unglamorous work most owners never get around to:

  • Completing every field: hours, services, categories, attributes, service areas.
  • Adding current photos of the business, the team, and the work itself.
  • Writing professional responses to existing reviews — including the bad ones.
  • Setting up a routine so new reviews get answered instead of piling up.
  • Cleaning up inconsistent information across Google's ecosystem.
