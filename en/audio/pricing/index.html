FAQs

Everything you need to know about haoword.audio credits, plans, and subscriptions.

What are credits and how are they deducted?

haoword.audio uses a credit system. Different tools deduct credits based on text length, audio duration, the selected mode, or a fixed cost per task. The editor shows an estimate before you submit, and qualifying failed tasks automatically restore the deducted credits.

Can I try it without subscribing?

Yes. After the tools officially launch, new users receive trial credits on signup, so you can try haoword.audio voice and audio tools before buying a plan. Each use still consumes credits at the rate shown on the tool page.

Do credits expire?

Yes. Annual plan credits are valid for 12 months; monthly plan credits are issued each month and valid for that month. Complimentary or promotional credits may carry a separate validity period — check the Plan & Credits page in your account for exact dates.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. After launch, you can cancel from the Plan & Credits page in your account. It will not renew again, paid access generally remains available through the end of the current billing period, and purchased credits stay valid until their displayed expiration date.

What is the difference between monthly and annual plans?

Monthly plans are billed monthly with credits issued each renewal period. Annual plans charge the full yearly amount upfront and issue the full annual credit allowance at once. The price shown on each annual card is the effective monthly rate while billing occurs annually — annual saves about 20% versus monthly billing.

What if the included credits are not enough?

You can choose a plan with a larger credit allowance. If the listed plans still do not cover your team or high-volume workflow, you can contact our team after launch to discuss an allowance that better fits your usage.

Can I use generated audio in commercial projects?

Generated audio may be used in eligible projects when you hold the necessary rights to the input content, reference voices, and related materials and comply with the current service terms and applicable law. Review the latest terms and usage restrictions before using a result in advertising, client work, or another commercial context.
