FAQ

Which tool should I use for narration, dialogue, or audiobooks?

Use Text to Speech for solo narration, Text to Dialogue for multi-character conversations, and the Voice Studio for long-form content that needs repeated revision — organize scripts, assign voices, and preview on a timeline.

Can I try it for free? Do I need to sign in?

Once tools launch, sample demos and built-in voices are available without signing in. AI generation tools require sign-in and consume credits; new users receive trial credits on registration.

How do credits work for AI generation?

Billing is based on text length, audio duration, or number of generations. The estimated cost is shown before each generation. See the pricing page for more credit options.

Can I use my own voice for voiceovers?

Yes. Voice Cloning accepts your own recordings or authorized voice samples. Clear vocals with minimal background noise produce the most natural results.

What audio formats and size limits apply to uploads?

Common formats like MP3, WAV, and M4A are supported. Duration and size limits vary by tool — check the upload area of the tool you choose.

Where do I download results? Can I keep editing later?

Preview and download directly on the tool page; past tasks live in History; Voice Studio projects can be reopened and edited. Generated files have a retention period, so download important work promptly.
