Cookie Policy

What cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, measure usage, and — where applicable — deliver relevant advertising. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and device identifiers.

Cookies we use

1. Strictly necessary cookies

These are required for basic site functionality, such as remembering cookie-consent choices and keeping the site secure. They do not track you for advertising and cannot be disabled without breaking core features.

2. Analytics cookies — Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-3LV3GH9T5X) to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are popular and how long people stay. Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visitors. IP addresses are processed by Google under its own terms. This data helps us improve content and is not used to identify you personally.

3. Advertising cookies

We display ads through Google AdSense and similar partners. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted by law and by your consent choices. Depending on your region, you may be shown a consent prompt before non-essential advertising cookies are set.

4. Affiliate tracking cookies

When you click an affiliate link (for example, to Codashop via our affiliate network), the network may set a cookie to attribute a later purchase to our referral. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

5. Third-party cookies

Embedded content and third-party services (such as video players, social share buttons, or analytics) may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies; please check the respective provider's cookie policy.

How to manage or disable cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Block all cookies: every modern browser lets you block third-party cookies or all cookies in settings. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of the site.
  • Delete cookies: you can clear existing cookies at any time through your browser's privacy settings.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or use your browser's "Do Not Track" / Global Privacy Control signal where supported.
  • Ad personalization: manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
