Campaign Protagonist

🎖️ Park Hyun-joon — Campaign Protagonist

The campaign protagonist of Modern Warfare 4, a young South Korean soldier. Set on the Korean Peninsula, players experience modern warfare through Park Hyun-joon's eyes. The first campaign chapter teaches cover, peeking, and basic tactics.

Weapon System (33 Weapons)

33 launch weapons spanning assault rifles, SMGs, LMGs, sniper rifles, pistols, shotguns, and more. Pick five favorites to level up — don't try to max all 33.

🔫 Assault Rifles

The mid-range workhorses with balanced range and damage. Fit most multiplayer maps.

💨 SMGs

Strong close-range burst with high mobility. Great for close-quarters maps like Kill Block.

🎯 Sniper Rifles

One-shot kills at long range. Learn each map's sniper lanes.

🔧 Gunny Loadout System

Switch between close-range, balanced, and long-range weapon setups with one tap. No manual re-kitting needed — a huge efficiency boost.

✨ Apex Attachments

Unlocked when a weapon reaches max level, granting unique effects without using regular attachment slots.

Weapon Picking Tips

  • New players: Start with one assault rifle as your main; learn the gunplay before trying other classes.
  • Focus upgrades: Level five favorite weapons instead of spreading progress thin.
  • Use Gunny: Quick-swap setups to adapt to different maps and ranges.
  • Try before you buy: Test every newly unlocked weapon at the firing range to learn its feel before taking it into matches.
