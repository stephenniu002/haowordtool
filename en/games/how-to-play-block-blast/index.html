How to Play Block Blast! — Beginner Guide & Tips

8x8 board rules, combo mechanics, 3x3 reserves, and beginner tips explained.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

Before You Start

Block Blast! is an 8x8 block-clearing puzzle game. The rules are simple, but high scores come from keeping your combo multiplier alive — not from one big clear.

How to Play — Step-by-Step Guide

1
Open the game

Download from the iOS or Android app store, or open the web version. No registration needed.

2
Learn the board

The board is an 8x8 grid of squares, starting empty.

3
Place blocks

Each round gives you three blocks of different shapes. Drag a block onto the board to place it.

4
Fill full rows or columns

Fill any complete row or column and it clears automatically.

5
Place all three blocks

You must place all three current blocks before the next set refreshes.

6
Keep your combo going

Clear at least once for several turns in a row to raise your combo multiplier.

7
Avoid breaking the combo

If a turn clears nothing, the combo multiplier resets to zero.

8
Reserve a 3x3 space

The 3x3 square is the biggest threat. Always keep one clean 3x3 area on the board.

9
Control board occupancy

Keep the board around 25% full — enough space to work with, and a base to keep combos going.

10
Game over condition

The game ends when your remaining blocks can't be placed anywhere.

Core Mechanics

8x8 board

The board is fixed at 8x8 squares, 64 cells total.

Three blocks per round

Each round gives you three non-rotatable blocks. A new set only appears after you place all three.

Row / column clears

Fill a complete row or column and it clears immediately.

Combo multiplier

Clear at least once for several turns in a row to raise the multiplier. One missed turn resets it.

The 3x3 threat

The 3x3 square needs 9 connected empty cells. Without a reserved area, it can end your game instantly.

No time limit

There is no timer — take your time and plan.

Beginner Tips

  1. Clear at least once every turn. Keeping the combo matters more than one big clear.
  2. Keep the board around 25% full — not too crowded, not too empty.
  3. Always reserve one clean 3x3 area.
  4. Fill from the edges toward the center; handle corners first.
  5. Plan all three blocks before placing any of them.

FAQ

How do you play Block Blast!?

Place three blocks on an 8x8 board, filling full rows or columns to clear them. A new set only appears after you place all three blocks.

Can you rotate blocks in Block Blast!?

No. Blocks cannot be rotated or flipped. This is a core limitation of the game.

How do you get a high score in Block Blast!?

The key is keeping your combo multiplier alive. Clearing at least once every turn matters more than one big clear.

Does Block Blast! have a leaderboard?

No official global leaderboard. Hungry Studio deliberately designed it as a casual game.

How many difficulty levels does Block Blast! have?

No difficulty options. Difficulty scales dynamically with your combo and board state.

Is Block Blast! free?

Free. Available on iOS and Android with no forced in-app purchases.
