Sprunki Mixing Tutorial — Build Your First Track Step by Step

A beginner-friendly walkthrough: from an empty stage to a finished, shareable mix.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

What You Will Learn

This tutorial walks you through building your first complete Sprunki track from an empty stage to a finished, shareable mix. No music theory needed — just your ears and a browser.

Build Your First Track — Step by Step

1
Open the game in your browser

Go to a fan site like sprunki.com. The game loads instantly — no download, no account, no install.

2
Meet the character roster

Look at the characters along the bottom. Each one is a sound: beats, bass lines, melodies, vocals, or effects. Hover or tap to preview if your version supports it.

3
Place your first beat

Drag one beat character onto the stage. This is your foundation — every good track starts with rhythm. Oren is a popular first pick.

4
Add low end

Drag on a bass-like or low melody character. It should sit underneath the beat, not fight it. If it sounds muddy, try a different character.

5
Layer a lead melody

Add one melodic character on top. This is the part listeners will hum later, so pick a sound you genuinely like.

6
Add flavor with vocals or effects

One vocal or effect character adds personality. Less is more — a single well-placed effect beats three competing ones.

7
Balance the mix

Click characters to mute them one by one. If muting something makes the track better, remove it. Aim for 4–6 active characters, not a full stage.

8
Create sections

Swap one or two characters mid-track to fake a verse/chorus structure. Even simple changes keep listeners engaged.

9
Listen back critically

Mute everything, then re-add characters one at a time. You will hear which sounds earn their place.

10
Record and share

Use your device's screen recorder, capture 30–60 seconds of your best section, and post it to TikTok or YouTube Shorts.

Beginner Mixing Rules of Thumb

DoDon't
Start with rhythm, then melodyStack all characters at once
Leave sonic space between soundsUse two similar melodies together
Mute and compare constantlyAssume more characters = better
Build in sections (swap characters)Keep the same full mix for minutes
Record your experimentsForget the combo that sounded great

FAQ

Do I need music theory to mix in Sprunki?

No. Sprunki is built for experimentation — drag characters, listen, and keep what sounds good. Your ears are the only theory you need.

How many characters should be on stage at once?

For beginners, 4–6 active characters is the sweet spot. More than that usually turns muddy unless you are deliberately building a climax.

Why does my mix sound messy?

Almost always too many competing sounds. Mute characters one by one until it cleans up, then rebuild more carefully.

Can I save my Sprunki mix?

Most fan versions do not have built-in saving, so screen-record anything you like — recordings double as shareable content.

What should I do after my first track?

Try the same process in a different phase or mod, hunt for combos, and check the advanced guide when you are ready for arrangement techniques.
