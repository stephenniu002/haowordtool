A quick orientation

Robux are simple. Free-Robux sites are scams.

Robux is the virtual currency of Roblox. Players spend it on avatar items, game passes, and private servers. It is bought with real money — every website promising free Robux is a scam designed to steal accounts.

This page is an independent guide. It does not sell Robux and never asks for your Roblox login. Prices below are reference points; always confirm the final total at checkout.

Username check: Robux purchases and gift cards apply to a specific username. One wrong letter sends your purchase to a stranger's account — and support cannot always reverse it.

Common questions

Roblox FAQ

Can you get free Robux?

Only tiny legitimate routes exist: Premium payouts, selling creations, or official events. Every 'free Robux generator' site is a scam.

Is Roblox Premium worth it?

For regular buyers, usually yes — monthly Robux plus 10% bonus on extra purchases often beats one-time packs at the same spend.

Do Robux work on all devices?

Yes. Robux are tied to your Roblox account, not your device, and sync across PC, mobile and console.

Do Robux expire?

No. They stay in your account until spent.

No. This is an independent informational guide. It does not sell Robux or collect account credentials.
