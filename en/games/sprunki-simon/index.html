Character Overview

Simon is one of the most beloved characters in Sprunki — a bright yellow figure with an infectious, cheerful energy. Fans love Simon for the fast, upbeat sounds that instantly lift any mix, making this character a go-to pick for energetic tracks.

Visually, Simon stands out with a sunny yellow design and an always-happy expression. In the community, Simon is frequently featured in fan art and remix videos, cementing a status as one of Sprunki's true fan favorites.

Sound & Role in Your Mix

💛 Sound profile

Simon delivers fast, bouncy, upbeat loops — think bright synth stabs and playful rhythmic patterns. The sound sits in the upper-mid range, cutting through a mix without overpowering the beat layer.

🎯 Role in your mix

Simon works best as an energy layer: drop Simon on top of a solid beat (Oren) and bass (Gray) foundation to add momentum and brightness. Because the loops are fast, Simon pairs well with slower, groovier characters to create contrast.

Normal vs Horror Mode

Normal mode

In normal mode, Simon is pure sunshine — bouncy animations, a big smile, and cheerful loops that make every mix feel alive.

Horror mode

Simon's horror transformation is one of the most striking in the game. The cheerful yellow design twists into something unsettling, and the once-playful loops distort into eerie, off-kilter patterns. It's a fan-favorite transformation precisely because the contrast with normal Simon is so extreme.

Combo Tips with Simon

  • Energy starter: Oren (beat) + Simon (energy) + Clukr (melody) — a bright, complete beginner mix.
  • Party mix: Simon + Raddy + Fun Bot — maximum upbeat energy for dance-style tracks.
  • Contrast play: Simon + Gray — fast cheerful loops over deep calm bass creates satisfying tension.
  • Horror trigger: Simon is often part of horror-phase combos; pair with Owakcx to explore the dark side.
