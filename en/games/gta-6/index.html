At a Glance

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) is the open-world action-adventure game from Rockstar Games, launching November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Set in the fictional state of Leonida and its core city Vice City, you play as dual protagonists Jason and Lucia. It's the first mainline GTA since GTA 5 in 2013 — a gap of over 13 years.

Release Date Confirmed

GTA 6 launches worldwide on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PS4 version. The PS5 Pro gets an enhanced performance mode.

Pre-order Bonuses

Everyone who pre-orders or buys before November 20, 2026 gets the Vintage Vice City Pack: the '55 Willard Faction car with garage, exclusive outfits and hairstyles, weapon liveries, and more. Digital pre-orders also include one month of GTA+.

Preload Timing

Digital preloading opens November 12, 2026 — a full week before launch. Physical editions (download code only, no disc) go on sale the same day so you can preload early.

PC Version Status

As of October 2026, Rockstar has announced nothing about a PC version. There is no official Steam page — you can't wishlist it, and no PC pre-orders are open. Take-Two's CEO has called PC "an increasingly important platform" but gave no GTA 6 PC timeline.

Performance

Per Rockstar's extended demo footage, GTA 6 runs at 30 FPS on standard consoles, with a 40 FPS mode on PS5 Pro. The demo was captured on a base PS5.

Map & World

The State of Leonida

GTA 6 is set in Leonida, anchored by Vice City. Rockstar confirms Leonida is roughly twice the size of GTA 5's map — the largest game world the studio has ever built.

Six Regions

Leonida contains six distinct regions, from highways and city streets to swamps and beaches.

Dynamic Weather

The game features a dynamic hurricane and localized weather physics system: localized rainfall, tropical storms, hurricanes, standing water — even rainbows.

Protagonists & Story

Jason Duval & Lucia Caminos

The game uses a dual-protagonist setup — you control Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a Bonnie-and-Clyde-style duo.

Story Core

After a simple job goes wrong, Jason and Lucia get pulled into a criminal conspiracy spanning Leonida. Their relationship develops through shared activities — pool, restaurants, bike rides, heists — and texting each other on their phones.

Confirmed Core Gameplay

  • Full 3D map exploration — the entire world map is explorable in 3D
  • 170+ animal species — massively upgraded animal rendering
  • Car theft minigame — stealing cars is now a lockpicking minigame
  • Deeper interior interaction — open fridge doors and pick specific items
  • Body changes — bulk up or gain weight at the gym
  • Livestreaming system — host full livestreams for your in-game fans
  • 600,000+ animations — recorded by the development team
  • Upgraded NPC reactions — more varied, more realistic responses

Online Mode Status

GTA 6 launches with single-player content only — no built-in multiplayer module. Take-Two says it will keep supporting GTA Online, but GTA 6's online mode has no announced timeline. Industry consensus: GTA 6 Online won't arrive before 2027 at the earliest.

Collector's Box

Rockstar's GTA 6 Collector's Box — "The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection" costs $399.99 and does not include the game — buy the game separately. Rockstar confirms the box ships without a disc; physical game copies are download-code only.
