How to Play Subway Surfers — Beginner Guide & Tips

Swipe controls, hoverboard usage, coin collecting, and secret star routes explained.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

Before You Start

Subway Surfers is SYBO's endless runner. You guide your character along subway tracks, dodging obstacles and collecting coins and power-ups. The core is your score multiplier and hoverboard endurance.

How to Play — Step-by-Step Guide

1
Launch the game

Download from the iOS or Android app store. No registration needed.

2
Pick a character

Beginners should try Tricky — earn coins while learning how the Pogo Stick works.

3
Start your first run

The game starts automatically. Your character runs forward while you dodge obstacles.

4
Learn the swipe controls

Swipe left/right to change lanes, swipe up to jump, swipe down to roll or drop fast.

5
Collect coins

Coins buy characters, hoverboards and power-up upgrades.

6
Use the hoverboard

The hoverboard is a "second life" — it takes one hit for you when you crash.

7
Learn jump canceling

Swipe down mid-jump to cancel it and land early — great for grabbing coins and power-ups.

8
Upgrade your score multiplier

Raise your multiplier by upgrading characters, completing daily missions and equipping boosters.

9
Collect secret stars

Secret stars only count if you touch them yourself — up to 3 per run.

10
Game over condition

The run ends when you crash with no hoverboard active.

Use power-upsTap the power-up icon

Core Mechanics

Endless runner

No finish line — the farther you run, the higher your score.

Three-lane system

Switch between three lanes, swipe up to jump, swipe down to roll.

Hoverboard system

The hoverboard acts as a second life — crashing consumes the board instead of ending the run.

Score multiplier

Raised by upgrading characters, completing daily missions and equipping boosters — up to 124x.

Secret stars

Up to 3 per run, and you must touch them yourself for them to count.

World Tour

Regular updates with different city themes, like Bali, Transylvania and more.

Beginner Tips

  1. Get comfortable with the swipe controls before chasing high scores.
  2. Cancel jumps with a downward swipe to land early and grab coins and power-ups.
  3. Don't burn your hoverboard the moment you get one — save it for high-speed sections or dense obstacle stretches.
  4. Early on, prioritize coin efficiency — upgrading key power-ups beats chasing scores.
  5. Try to collect all 3 secret stars every run.

FAQ

How do you play Subway Surfers?

Swipe left/right to change lanes, swipe up to jump, swipe down to roll. Collect coins and power-ups, dodge obstacles — the farther you run, the higher your score.

What's the best character for beginners in Subway Surfers?

Try Tricky — earn coins while learning how the Pogo Stick works.

What's the best way to use hoverboards in Subway Surfers?

The hoverboard is a "second life" — don't burn it the moment you get one. Save it for high-speed sections or dense obstacle stretches.

How do you get secret stars in Subway Surfers?

Secret stars only count if you touch them yourself — the magnet can't grab them. Usually up to 3 per run.

How many modes does Subway Surfers have?

Mainly the endless runner mode, plus World Tour updates and limited-time events.

Is Subway Surfers free?

Free. Available on iOS and Android with optional in-app purchases.
