Character Tips

Beginners: Start with Tricky — earn coins while learning the pogo stick. Stick with Jake while you're short on coins.

Mid-game: Once you have enough coins, unlock Fresh or Yutani for balanced performance. Spike's skateboarding dog animation is highly distinctive.

Event characters: Limited-time event characters are usually stronger but harder to obtain. They may not return once the event ends.

World Tour exclusives: Each World Tour brings exclusive characters. Lestari and Golden Guardian from the Bali tour are 2026's standouts.
