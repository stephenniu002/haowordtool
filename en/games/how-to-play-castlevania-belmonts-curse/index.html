How to Play Castlevania: Belmont's Curse — Combat, Weapons & Beginner Guide

Holy Light parry, whip exploration, weapon systems, and beginner tips explained.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

Before You Start

Belmont's Curse is the first 2D mainline Castlevania in 18 years. You play as Rose Belmont, daughter of Trevor Belmont. The core is Metroidvania exploration plus the Holy Light parry.

How to Play — Step-by-Step Guide

1
Choose a Difficulty

On first launch, pick Standard, Hellwalker, or 66 Days mode. Beginners should pick Standard.

2
Complete the Opening Tutorial

The prologue teaches basic movement, attacks, jumps, and parries.

3
Practice the Holy Light Parry

This is the game's core defensive mechanic. Enemies telegraph attacks — a well-timed parry negates damage and lets you counter.

4
Learn the Whip's Three Uses

The whip attacks, grapples, and swings. Grapple to close distance; swing to reach platforms jumping can't.

5
Collect Main Weapons

The game includes 30+ main weapons — whips, swords, hammers, and more. Hold to equip a newly picked-up weapon.

6
Learn Sub-Weapons

25+ sub-weapons include great axes, bows, fire-breathing lion heads, and more.

7
Use Reforge Charms

Upgraded the wrong weapon? Reforge Charms reset upgrades and refund all materials.

8
Equip Arcana

Arcana grant passive bonuses — equip up to 3 at once.

9
Explore Hidden Areas

Use the whip's grapple and swing to reach platforms regular jumps can't.

10
Collect Hero Heart & Sage Heart Shards

These shards grant permanent bonuses — grab as many as you can on every map.

Core Mechanics

Holy Light Parry

Enemies telegraph attacks. A successful parry negates damage and lets you counter — it's the core defensive mechanic.

Three Whip Uses

Attack, grapple, and swing. Grapple for combat, swing for exploration.

Weapon System

30+ main weapons and 25+ sub-weapons. Hold to equip a newly picked-up weapon directly.

Reforge Charms

Reset an upgraded weapon and reclaim all materials — experiment freely with different combos.

Arcana

Passive bonuses, up to 3 equipped at once.

66 Days Mode

A higher-challenge difficulty where death costs progress.

Beginner Tips

  1. Master the Holy Light parry first — it's the most important defensive mechanic in the demo.
  2. The whip's grapple and swing are the core of exploration — use them to find hidden areas.
  3. Hold to equip a newly picked-up weapon directly — no menu needed.
  4. Reforge Charms reset upgrades and refund materials, so experiment with different weapons freely.
  5. Equip up to 3 Arcana at once, and swap them based on enemy types.

FAQ

How do I play Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?

A 2D exploration action game (Metroidvania). You play as Rose Belmont, exploring Paris and Dracula's castle. The core is the Holy Light parry and whip combat.

How many difficulty levels does Belmont's Curse have?

Standard, Hellwalker, and 66 Days mode.

Does Belmont's Curse support Chinese?

Yes. It includes Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and English subtitles.

Does Belmont's Curse have a demo?

Yes. A demo launched October 1, 2026, with saves carrying over to the full game.

How do I switch weapons in Belmont's Curse?

Hold to equip a newly picked-up weapon directly — no menu needed.

Is Belmont's Curse a Metroidvania?

Yes. It's a 2D exploration action game, and the first 2D mainline Castlevania in 18 years.
