Sprunki Black Hat — What It Does & How to Trigger Horror Mode

Everything about the Black Hat: the item that flips Sprunki into Horror Mode, and how to use it correctly.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

What Is the Black Hat?

The Black Hat is a special trigger item in Sprunki — usually shown as a black top hat icon in the selection area. It isn't a musical character: it's the switch that activates Horror Mode, transforming every character on stage into their dark, corrupted forms and shifting the audio to eerie, distorted versions.

It originated as the community's standard horror trigger and appears in most mainline builds and phase mods. If a Sprunki version has a horror mode, the Black Hat (or a phase-specific equivalent) is almost always how you activate it.

How to Use the Black Hat

1
Locate the Black Hat icon

Look in the bottom character/item bar for a black top hat. It's usually separated from the musical characters.

2
Drag it onto the stage

Drag it exactly like a character and drop it anywhere on the stage.

3
Watch the transformation

All characters morph into horror forms — visuals darken, sounds distort. This is Horror Mode.

4
Keep building your mix

Horror sounds still layer. Add or remove characters to compose in the corrupted style.

5
Remove it to revert

Drag the hat off or reset the stage to return to normal mode in most builds.

What Exactly Changes

ElementNormal modeAfter Black Hat
CharactersBright, cheerful designsDark, corrupted horror redesigns
BackgroundColorful stageDarkened, ominous atmosphere
SoundsUpbeat loopsEerie, distorted versions of the same loops
MixingStandard layeringLayering still works — now with horror sounds

Troubleshooting

  • No Black Hat icon? Your build may use a different trigger — some mods use specific character combos instead. Check that version's guide.
  • Hat does nothing? Try dropping it in the center of the stage, or on a specific character — behavior varies by build.
  • Only one character changed? Some builds support per-character transformation: drag the hat directly onto a single character.
  • Can't revert? Use the stage reset button to return to normal mode.

Tips

  • Build first, transform second: compose a full normal mix, then drop the hat for maximum before/after drama.
  • The morph is the money shot: screen-record the exact transformation moment for Shorts and TikTok.
  • Compare across phases: the hat exists in most phases, but each phase's horror designs differ.

FAQ

What does the Black Hat do in Sprunki?

It triggers Horror Mode — transforming all characters into dark corrupted forms and shifting sounds to eerie versions.

How do you use the Black Hat in Sprunki?

Drag the black top hat icon from the selection bar onto the stage, just like placing a character.

Why isn't the Black Hat working?

Some builds use different triggers (character combos instead). Try centering the drop, or check that version's specific trigger method.

Can you turn off Horror Mode after using the Black Hat?

Usually yes — remove the hat or reset the stage to return to normal mode.

Does every Sprunki mod have the Black Hat?

Most mainline builds and phase mods do, but some mods replace it with their own trigger combos.
