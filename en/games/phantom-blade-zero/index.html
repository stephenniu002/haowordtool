At a Glance

Phantom Blade Zero is a third-person wuxia action RPG developed by China's S-Game, built on Unreal Engine 5. You play as Soul, an elite assassin framed for murder with only 66 days left to live, fighting to survive in the martial-arts underworld of the "Phantom World" and uncover a conspiracy. Launches October 29, 2026 on PS5 and PC.

Release Date & Platforms

Phantom Blade Zero launches worldwide on October 29, 2026 for PS5, PS5 Pro, and PC. The PC version is available via Steam, Epic Games Store, and WeGame. Originally scheduled for September 9, 2026, it was delayed ~50 days to October 29 — the official explanation: to make sure the launch version is the strongest possible, rather than relying on post-launch patches.

The Development Team

Developed and published in-house by Beijing's S-Game on Unreal Engine 5. Founder Liang Qiwei (Soulframe Liang) serves as game director and producer. Tencent acquired a 25% stake in S-Game in 2021. The team has publicly refused generative AI, promising that "every piece of content is handcrafted by real artists."

Protagonist & Story

You play as Soul, once the top assassin of the mysterious "Order." Betrayed by a colleague and framed for assassinating the Order's leader, he is impaled through the heart in a duel with his sworn brother Zuo Shang. The mysterious healer "Ghosthand" saves Soul's life — at a terrible price: he has only 66 days left. Soul must use his limited time to survive the chaos of the martial world and unravel a conspiracy that could upend the entire wulin.

Donnie Yen's Involvement

Kung fu star Donnie Yen serves as creative consultant, involved since 2023, lending his martial arts and action-film expertise to the game's combat and action design. He also plays the key character Mó Yuan via facial and motion capture.

Core Gameplay

Weapon System

Unlock 30+ main weapons and 25 sub-weapons called "Phantom Edges". Weapons can be upgraded and paired with different accessories to build your own combat style. Some blades use drunken-fist styles, some counter aerial attacks, and twin blades can fire projectiles.

"Kungfupunk" Combat

The team calls its combat system "Kungfupunk" — traditional swordsmanship fused with fast-paced martial arts and modern rock music. Combat revolves around a health bar and a "killing intent" (Sha Qi) gauge, built around "high-speed combat + deep progression," including a dual-weapon switch system.

Weapon Reforging

Never fear upgrading the wrong weapon. The game lets you reforge weapons, recovering all materials to reinvest in another weapon — encouraging experimentation with different builds.

Beginner Tips

  1. Learn reforging early — don't fear upgrading the "wrong" weapon. Reforging returns all materials, so experiment boldly with different weapons.
  2. Pick the right difficulty — start on Wayfarer to learn the story and combat rhythm, then step up. 66 Days Mode is for hardcore players only.
  3. Explore side quests in the "Phantom World" — eight locations including Panzhen and the unique "Illusion Realm." Side quests can unlock new story paths.
  4. Use the summon system — summons assist in combat and can take down enemies from range. Great for tough boss fights.
  5. Check PC requirements — minimum: Intel Core i7-8700K / AMD Ryzen 5 3600, 16GB RAM, NVIDIA GTX 1660 6GB. 4K 60FPS with ray tracing needs an RTX 5080 with DLSS.

FAQ

When does Phantom Blade Zero release?

October 29, 2026, worldwide — on PS5 and PC.

Is Phantom Blade Zero a Soulslike?

No. The developers describe it as a wuxia action RPG, not a Soulslike. Combat emphasizes fast-paced swordplay and cinematic martial arts rather than Soulslike's high-punishment loop.

Does Phantom Blade Zero support Chinese?

Yes. The game includes Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

Who is the protagonist of Phantom Blade Zero?

Soul — a framed elite assassin with only 66 days left to live. Donnie Yen is creative consultant and plays Mó Yuan.

How much does Phantom Blade Zero cost?

Standard Edition $59.99, Digital Deluxe $69.99.

Is there a PC version of Phantom Blade Zero?

Yes. The PC version launches day one via Steam, Epic Games Store, and WeGame.

How many difficulty modes does Phantom Blade Zero have?

Four: Wayfarer (cinematic), Pathbreaker (balanced), Hellwalker (hard), and 66 Days Mode (permadeath).
