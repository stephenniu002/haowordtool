UC is useful. Your character ID is essential.

Unknown Cash (UC) is PUBG Mobile's premium currency, spent on crates, outfits, weapon skins, and the Royale Pass. Most top-ups need your character ID rather than your login password.

This page is an independent guide. It does not process payments and never asks for login details. Prices vary by country, so always confirm the final total at checkout.

How to find your PUBG Mobile character ID

1

Open your profile

Launch PUBG Mobile and tap your avatar in the top-left corner.

2

Find the ID

Your numeric character ID appears below your nickname on the profile screen.

3

Copy it exactly

Long-press to copy, then double-check the digits before pasting into any top-up page.

4

Match the nickname

Confirm the nickname shown at checkout matches your account before paying.

What does UC look like?

Your character ID is a numeric identifier shown on your profile. Length varies between accounts, so don't rely on a fixed digit count. If any page asks for your password, recovery email or login code, leave immediately — a normal ID-based top-up never needs those.

Common questions

PUBG Mobile FAQ

Do I need my password to top up UC?

No. A standard top-up needs your character ID and a payment method — never your password.

What is the difference between PUBG Mobile and BGMI?

BGMI is the India-specific version with separate servers and top-up routes. Make sure you use the top-up page matching your game version.

Why hasn't my UC arrived?

Check the order status, receipt and character ID first. If payment succeeded but UC is missing, contact the store's official support.

Are UC sellers on social media safe?

No. Sellers advertising cheap UC in chats or comments are almost always scams or stolen-payment resellers risking your account.

Is this an official Level Infinite page?

No. This is an independent informational guide. It does not sell UC or collect account credentials.
