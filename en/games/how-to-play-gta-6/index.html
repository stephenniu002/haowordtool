How to Play GTA 6 — Controls, Characters & Beginner Guide

Dual protagonist switching, controls, core mechanics, and beginner tips explained.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

Before You Start

GTA 6 is the series' first dual-protagonist open-world game — switch between Jason and Lucia anytime. After launch, this page will be updated with hands-on controls. For now, here is everything confirmed from official announcements.

How to Play — Step-by-Step Guide

1
Launch the Game

Launch GTA 6 on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S and complete the first-boot brightness, HDR, and subtitle setup.

2
Complete the Opening Tutorial

The game starts with a prologue that walks you through basic movement, shooting, and driving. Just follow the prompts.

3
Learn Character Switching

With dual protagonists, you can switch between Jason and Lucia anytime during free roam. Switching is usually available when the two are close together or the story allows it.

4
Get Familiar with Movement

Left stick moves, right stick controls the camera. Hold the sprint button to run, press crouch to sneak.

5
Get Familiar with Driving

After entering any vehicle, RT/R2 accelerates, LT/L2 brakes/reverses, left stick steers. Controller vibration gives you road feedback.

6
Learn the Shooting System

LT/L2 to aim, RT/R2 to fire. Press LB/L1 to take cover — you can blind-fire or pop out to shoot.

7
Manage Your Wanted Level

Committing crimes raises your wanted level, up to 6 stars. Escape by leaving police sight, switching cars, entering the subway, or hiding out.

8
Use Your Phone and Map

Your phone receives missions and lets you take photos and livestream. The map sets waypoints and shows mission markers and saved locations.

9
Complete Side Activities

The gym, pool, restaurants, and ride-sharing are core side activities that affect character stats and relationship growth.

10
Save Your Progress

The game autosaves, and you can also save manually at safehouses. We recommend a manual save before major missions.

Core Mechanics

Dual Protagonist System

Jason and Lucia each have independent stats, skills, and gear. Some missions can only be triggered by a specific protagonist.

Wanted Level

Up to 6 stars. Higher levels bring more aggressive police pursuit.

Dynamic Weather

Includes localized rain, tropical storms, hurricanes, standing water, and rainbows.

Body Shape

Bulking up at the gym or overeating at restaurants changes your build, affecting stamina and appearance.

Livestream System

Host full livestreams for yourself — audience reactions affect in-game earnings.

Ride-Sharing

Summon a vehicle and ride with NPCs to trigger random dialogue and social events.

Beginner Tips

  1. Finish the prologue tutorial first and get a feel for movement and driving before free-roaming.
  2. Upgrade vehicles and weapons early — don't spend all your money on clothes.
  3. Watch for mission prompts when switching protagonists; some missions lock to the current character.
  4. At 3+ wanted stars, switching cars, entering tunnels, or taking the subway is the fastest escape.
  5. Side activities and random events affect character relationships and later story options.

FAQ

How do I switch protagonists in GTA 6?

During free roam, hold down on the D-pad (or hold Tab) to open the character switcher and pick Jason or Lucia. Missions usually lock you to the current character.

How do I lose a wanted level in GTA 6?

Switch cars, enter subway tunnels, break police line of sight, or use hideouts to lower your wanted level.

Does GTA 6 have a tutorial?

Yes. The prologue walks you through movement, shooting, and driving.

Does GTA 6 support Chinese?

Yes. It includes Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

Can I play GTA 6 offline?

The single-player campaign can be played offline. Online mode launches after release.

Can I use both controller and keyboard?

PS5 and Xbox use their controllers; the PC version hasn't been announced. Rockstar hasn't revealed PC controls yet.
