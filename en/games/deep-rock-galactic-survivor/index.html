First dive

Keep moving, keep mining.

In a survivor-like, positioning beats firepower. These habits carry over from PC and Xbox to your first PS5 dive.

Three habits for new players

  • 01
    Never stop moving.
    Your weapons fire on their own. Kiting the swarm in wide arcs keeps you alive longer than standing your ground.
  • 02
    Mine between waves.
    Ore is your upgrade fuel. Carve through walls while the swarm thins — resources win runs.
  • 03
    Build around one weapon.
    Stack upgrades on a single gun instead of spreading thin. A maxed build clears the boss; three half-built ones do not.

When does Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor release on PS5?

October 7, 2026. The Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 versions follow on October 9, 2026.

Which classes can I play?

Four: Scout, Engineer, Gunner and Driller. The announced Knockout expansion adds a fifth class, the Brawler.

Is it multiplayer?

No. Survivor is strictly single-player, unlike the original Deep Rock Galactic which is a four-player co-op shooter.

What is the Knockout expansion?

An upcoming expansion adding the Brawler class, the Sandblasted Corridors biome and a high-risk mission with new objectives and a boss. No release date yet.
