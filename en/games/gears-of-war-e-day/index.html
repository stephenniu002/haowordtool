Gears of War: E-Day Guide Hub: Everything to Know Before Launch

Last updated: October 4, 2026

In One Sentence

Gears of War: E-Day is a third-person cover shooter developed by The Coalition and People Can Fly, published by Xbox Game Studios. A prequel set 14 years before the original Gears of War (2006), it follows a younger Marcus Fenix and Dominic Santiago on Emergence Day — the day the Locust Horde first erupted from beneath the planet Sera — fighting for humanity's survival in the city of Kalona.

Release Date & Editions

The game launches worldwide on October 6, 2026 at 8:00 a.m. PT (15:00 UTC). Premium Edition ($99.99) owners get 5-day early access starting October 1. The Standard Edition lists at $69.99 on Steam (US).

Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass subscribers can play on day one, October 6, at no extra cost. Note: the Premium Edition cosmetic bundle (weapon skins, seasonal content, etc.) is not included with Game Pass.

Story: What Is E-Day?

"E-Day" stands for Emergence Day, the pivotal moment in Gears lore when the Locust Horde first erupted from underground, pushing humanity to the brink of extinction within hours. Set 14 years before the original game, E-Day follows Marcus and Dom — veterans of the Pendulum Wars just returned home — as Emergence Day unfolds. The developers describe a deliberate return to the series' darker, grittier survival-horror roots, treating the Locust as genuine monsters.

Gameplay Overview

  • Classic cover shooting: the series' signature cover system returns — take cover, pop out, blind-fire to suppress
  • Five-act campaign: story campaign in five acts, playable in online co-op
  • Horde Siege: rebuilt PvE wave-defense mode — hold the line with your squad against the Locust
  • Versus: PvP multiplayer modes
  • Unreal Engine 5: new destruction physics and advanced lighting; 4K / up to 60 FPS on Xbox Series X|S
  • Achievements: full achievement list confirmed, covering story, collectibles, and multiplayer progress
