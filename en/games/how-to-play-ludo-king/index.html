How to Play Ludo King — Beginner Guide & Tips

Dice rules, safe squares, token management, and blockade tactics explained.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

Before You Start

Ludo King is Gametion's classic Ludo mobile game, based on the ancient Indian game Pachisi. Roll the dice to move tokens — the first player to bring all four tokens home wins.

How to Play — Step-by-Step Guide

1
Pick a mode

On first launch, choose Classic or Quick mode. Beginners should start with Classic to learn the full rhythm.

2
Pick player count

Supports 2–6 player online matches, offline AI matches and local multiplayer.

3
Roll the dice

Tap the dice button to roll. You need a 6 to move a token out of the base.

4
Move a token out

When you roll a 6, tap a token in your base to move it to the start square.

5
Move your tokens

Tap a token on the board to move it by the dice value.

6
Rolling a 6 grants a bonus turn

Rolling a 6 gives you one extra roll.

7
Capture opponent tokens

Land on an opponent's token and it gets sent back to base.

8
Use safe squares

Star-marked safe squares and colored start squares protect your tokens from capture.

9
Build blockades

Stack two same-color tokens on one square to form a blockade opponents can't pass.

10
Get all four tokens home

The first player to bring all four tokens to the finish wins.

Core Mechanics

Rolling the dice

You need a 6 to move a token out, and rolling a 6 grants a bonus roll.

Capturing tokens

Land on an opponent's square and their token goes back to base.

Safe squares

Star-marked safe squares and colored start squares — tokens on them can't be captured.

Blockades

Two same-color tokens stacked on one square — opponents can't pass.

Home stretch

Tokens need an exact roll to enter the finish; overshooting bounces back.

Quick mode

Shorter animations and game length, perfect for fast matches.

Beginner Tips

  1. Spread your tokens out — don't push just one.
  2. When you roll a 6, opening a new token is usually the best move.
  3. Prioritize capturing the opponent's most advanced token.
  4. Place blockades on paths your opponents must take.
  5. Switch between attack and defense based on your roll — don't cling to one strategy.

FAQ

How do you play Ludo King?

Roll the dice to move tokens — the first player to bring all four tokens to the finish wins. You need a 6 to move a token out, and rolling a 6 grants a bonus roll.

How many players does Ludo King support?

Supports 2–6 player online matches, offline AI matches and local multiplayer.

Where are the safe squares in Ludo King?

Star-marked squares on the board are safe — tokens on them can't be captured. Colored start squares are safe too.

What's the difference between Quick and Classic mode in Ludo King?

Quick mode shortens animations and game length for faster matches. Classic mode keeps the full game rhythm.

How do you join tournaments in Ludo King?

Ludo King supports 8-player tournaments — winners can earn up to 6x the entry coins.

Is Ludo King free?

Free. Available on iOS, Android and PC with optional in-app purchases.
