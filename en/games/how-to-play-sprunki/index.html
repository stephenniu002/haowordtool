How to Play Sprunki — Beginner Guide & Tips

Character dragging, mix layering, horror phase triggers, and beginner tips explained.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

Before You Start

Sprunki is a fan-made music-mixing browser game based on Incredibox. The core idea: drag different characters onto the stage, layering beats, melodies and vocals to create your own mix.

How to Play — Step-by-Step Guide

1
Open the game

Open it directly on fan sites like sprunki.com. No download or registration needed.

2
Pick a phase

The game usually starts in the normal version. Different versions and mods have different phases.

3
Meet the characters

Common characters include Oren, Raddy, Clukr, Fun Bot, Vineria, Gray and more. Each character represents a sound.

4
Drag characters onto the stage

Drag a character from below onto the stage and it starts playing its sound.

5
Layer multiple characters

Stack multiple characters on the stage to build a complete mix.

6
Mute or remove characters

Click a character on the stage to mute or remove it.

7
Try specific combos

Specific character combinations trigger phase changes or hidden easter eggs.

8
Trigger the horror phase

Some combos enter the horror / corrupted phase — the visuals, sounds and character designs all change.

9
Record your mix

Screen-record and share to TikTok or YouTube Shorts — it picks up organic traffic easily.

10
Try different mods

Sprunki has tons of mod versions, including Phase 3, Phase 4, Wenda Treatment and more.

Core Mechanics

Character sound system

Each character represents a sound: beats, melodies, vocals or effects.

Mix layering

Multiple characters play at once, forming a complete mix.

Phase changes

Specific combos trigger phase changes, including the horror / corrupted phase.

Hidden easter eggs

Certain character combinations trigger hidden effects.

Mod versions

Countless fan-made mods, each with different characters and sounds.

Beginner Tips

  1. Listen to each character's sound on its own before deciding how to combine them.
  2. Keep the rhythm balanced — don't stack every character at once.
  3. Leave one character free for variation to create a sense of sections.
  4. Try triggering hidden phases — that's the core hook for short-video sharing.
  5. Screen-record and share to TikTok or YouTube Shorts — it picks up organic traffic easily.

FAQ

How do you play Sprunki?

Drag different characters onto the stage, layering beats, melodies and vocals to create your own mix. Click a character to mute or remove it.

Is Sprunki free?

Usually free — it plays right in the browser.

Can you play Sprunki on mobile?

Yes, most versions support mobile browsers.

Is Sprunki suitable for kids?

The normal phase is fine, but the horror phase may scare children.

How do you trigger the horror phase in Sprunki?

Specific character combinations trigger the horror / corrupted phase. Trigger conditions differ per mod.

Is Sprunki the same as Incredibox?

Similar gameplay, but Sprunki is fan-made, not the official Incredibox.
