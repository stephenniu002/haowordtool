Player ID basics

How to find your Free Fire MAX UID

1

Open your profile

Launch Free Fire MAX and tap your avatar or profile area.

2

Look below your nickname

Your numeric player ID should appear beside or under your profile details.

3

Copy and verify

Compare the UID with your in-game nickname before opening a top-up checkout.

What does a UID look like?

It is usually a numeric player identifier. The exact length may differ between accounts, so do not rely on a fixed number of digits.

UID: 1234567890

If a website asks for your password, recovery email, verification code or social login, stop and leave the page. A normal UID-based top-up should not require those secrets.

Protect your account

Three checks before you confirm payment

Check the domain

Look at the address bar carefully. Avoid pages with strange spelling, urgent countdowns or unexpected redirects.

Check the recipient

Confirm the in-game name and UID shown at checkout. A wrong UID may send the purchase to another account.

Check the receipt

Keep payment confirmation and order details until the diamonds appear. Contact the store through its official support channel.

Common questions

Free Fire MAX top-up FAQ

Do I need my Free Fire MAX password to buy diamonds?

Usually no. A standard UID-based top-up asks for the player ID and may require a payment method, but it should not require your game password.

Can I use any Codashop country page?

No. Store availability, payment methods and prices depend on the selected country or region. Use the region that matches your account and payment method when possible.

Why have my diamonds not arrived yet?

Check the order status, receipt and UID first. If payment succeeded but the balance has not updated, contact the store or publisher through an official support page.

Is this page an official Garena or Codashop page?

No. This is an independent informational guide. It does not sell diamonds, process payments or collect account credentials.
