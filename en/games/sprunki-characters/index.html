Character Details

🥁 Oren — Beat

One of Sprunki's core beat characters. Provides a steady rhythmic foundation — the first pick for most mixes. Ideal as the bottom layer of your track.

🎤 Raddy — Vocals

The main vocal character, delivering melodic vocal samples. Pairs with Oren to build a mix foundation quickly.

🎺 Clukr — Melody

A melody character with a bright tone — great as the high layer of a mix. Together with Oren and Raddy, the most recommended trio for beginners.

🤖 Fun Bot — Electronic

An electronic character with a mechanical, synth-like tone. Perfect for mixes that need a modern, electronic feel.

🌿 Vineria — Ambient

An ambient character providing nature-inspired sound samples. Works well as the background atmosphere layer.

🎹 Gray — Bass

The bass character, providing the low-frequency foundation. Bass is the key to a thick-sounding mix.

🎸 Brud — Rhythm

A rhythm character that adds punch to your mix. Pairs well with Oren to build the rhythm layer.

🎧 Garnold — Effects

An effects character providing special sounds. Great for adding accents and transitions to a mix.

👽 Owakcx — Special

A special character with a unique tone, often used for phase-change transitions. Frequently appears when triggering the horror phase.

☁️ Sky — Atmosphere

An atmosphere character with a soft background sound. Ideal for mix openings or transitions.

☀️ Mr. Sun — High Pitch

A high-pitch character with a bright tone — a great high-frequency accent. Adds extra dimension to your mix.

🌳 Mr. Tree — Low End

A low-frequency character that fills out the bottom of a mix. Pairs with Gray to build a complete low end.

Character Tips

Beginner combo: Oren + Raddy + Clukr. These three form the most basic mix structure: beat + vocals + melody. Add more characters once you're comfortable.

Advanced mixes: Add Gray (bass) and Brud (rhythm) to the core trio for a thicker sound.

Triggering the horror phase: Trigger conditions vary by mod. It usually requires a specific character combination — Owakcx and Garnold are often part of it.

Recording & sharing: Pick combos with strong rhythm and clear layers — they earn more organic traffic on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many characters does Sprunki have?

The official original has over 20 characters; different mod versions add new characters.

What is the best beginner character combo in Sprunki?

Oren + Raddy + Clukr is the recommended starter combo — the most basic mix structure.

How do you trigger the horror phase in Sprunki?

Specific character combinations trigger the horror/corrupted phase. Trigger conditions vary by mod.

Do Sprunki characters have official names?

Yes. Sprunki is a fan-made game, but character naming is fairly standardized.

Are Sprunki characters the same across mods?

No. Character names, appearances and sounds can differ between mods.
