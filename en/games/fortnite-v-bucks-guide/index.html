A quick orientation

V-Bucks are simple. Scams are not.

V-Bucks are Fortnite's in-game currency. Players spend them on outfits, emotes, gliders, pickaxes, wraps and the seasonal Battle Pass. They are bought with real money through official stores — there is no legitimate way to get large amounts of V-Bucks for free.

This page is an independent guide. It does not sell V-Bucks and never asks for your Epic login. Prices change over time, so always confirm the final total at checkout.

Wallet note: V-Bucks bought on most platforms share one wallet when you use the same Epic account, but some platform-specific purchases stay on that platform. Check Epic's current wallet rules if you play on multiple devices.

Common questions

Fortnite FAQ

Can you get free V-Bucks legitimately?

Small amounts can come from the Battle Pass free track and Save the World mode. Any site promising thousands of free V-Bucks is a scam.

Do V-Bucks transfer between platforms?

When you use one Epic account, V-Bucks generally share a wallet across PC, Xbox, cloud and Android, with some platform-specific exceptions. Check Epic's current rules if you play on multiple devices.

What is the cheapest way to buy V-Bucks?

Larger bundles have better per-unit value, and official sales are rare. Avoid third-party sellers — discounted V-Bucks from unofficial sources risk account penalties.

Do V-Bucks expire?

No. V-Bucks stay in your account until you spend them.

Is this an official Epic Games page?

No. This is an independent informational guide. It does not sell V-Bucks or collect account credentials.
