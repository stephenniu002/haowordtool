Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Characters

Rose Belmont, the weapon system, Arcana and the Holy Light parry — fully explained.

Main Characters

🌹 Rose Belmont — Protagonist

The protagonist of Belmont's Curse and daughter of Trevor Belmont. The first 2D mainline Castlevania protagonist in 18 years. She explores Paris and Dracula's castle, fighting with her whip and the Holy Light parry.

⚔️ Trevor Belmont — The Legendary Belmont

Rose's father and the legendary Belmont of the Castlevania series. He appears as a background figure in this game — Rose's spiritual guide.

Weapon System

🗡️ Main Weapons (30+)

Whips, swords, hammers and more. Hold to equip a newly picked-up weapon instantly — no menu needed. The whip has three uses: attack, grapple and swing.

🎯 Sub-Weapons (25)

Great axes, bows, fire-breathing lion heads and other "Shadow Arms". Some sub-weapons can be fired at range.

♻️ Reforge Charm

Resets an upgraded weapon and refunds all materials — encouraging you to experiment with different weapon builds.

✨ Arcana

Passive bonuses; equip up to 3 at once. Swap Arcana to match different enemy types.

Core Mechanics

🛡️ Holy Light Parry

The game's core defensive mechanic. Enemies telegraph their attacks — a well-timed parry negates damage and opens up a counterattack. The most important mechanic in the demo.

Frequently Asked Questions

The protagonist is Rose Belmont, daughter of Trevor Belmont.

Over 30 main weapons and 25 sub-weapons.

The game's core defensive mechanic. Enemies telegraph their attacks; a successful parry negates damage and enables a counterattack.

What is a Reforge Charm?

An item that resets an upgraded weapon and refunds all materials.
