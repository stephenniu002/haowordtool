⚔️ Soul — Protagonist

The protagonist of Phantom Blade Zero, a wuxia swordsman fighting across the Umbral world. Players guide Soul through eight locations (including Pang Town and the Illusion) in exploration and combat. Core gameplay: fast-paced Kungfupunk swordplay + sub-weapon switching.

Weapon System

The weapon system is the core of character building in Phantom Blade Zero. Over 30 main weapons and 25 Shadow Arsenal sub-weapons can be freely combined.

🗡️ Main Weapons (30+)

Including swords, blades, spears, and more. Main weapons define your basic attack style. Each weapon can be upgraded; maxing one out unlocks its Apex accessory.

🎯 Shadow Arsenal Sub-Weapons (25)

Including great axes, bows, flamethrowing lion heads, and more. Some sub-weapons can fire at range. Main and sub-weapons can be swapped anytime — watch weapon animations and cooldowns when switching.

♻️ Weapon Reforging

Don't worry about upgrading the wrong weapon. Use reforging to recover all materials and reinvest them in a new weapon. Experimenting with builds is encouraged.

✨ Apex Accessories

Unlocked when a weapon reaches max level, granting unique effects without using regular accessory slots. Key to late-game character power.

Weapon Picking Tips

  • New players: Pick a fast-attacking main weapon paired with a ranged Shadow Arsenal option, so you have both melee and ranged answers.
  • Advanced loadouts: Swap weapons to match boss traits. Reforging lets you adjust anytime — no fear of wrong picks.
  • Max out first: Max each weapon to unlock its Apex accessory before deciding whether to reforge, maximizing material efficiency.

The Shadow Arsenal is the sub-weapon system — 25 options including great axes, bows, and a flamethrowing lion head, some usable at range.
