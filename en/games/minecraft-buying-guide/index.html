A quick orientation

Two editions. One right choice for you.

Minecraft is sold in two editions: Java Edition for PC (Windows, Mac, Linux) and Bedrock Edition for consoles, mobile and Windows PC. They look similar but differ in mods, servers, cross-play and redstone behavior.

On PC, Mojang now sells both editions together as one bundle, so most new PC buyers don't need to choose. Console and mobile players only have the Bedrock option. This guide helps you buy the right copy once.

Key tip: If a deal looks far below the official price, check the seller carefully. Revoked or region-mismatched keys are the most common complaint with cheap Minecraft listings.

Avoid mistakes

Three checks before you pay

Match the region

A key locked to another region may not redeem on your account. Confirm region compatibility in the listing.

Beware account sellers

Listings selling 'accounts' instead of keys usually violate the terms of service and can be recovered by the original owner.

Keep your receipt

Save the order confirmation and key until the game is redeemed and running on your account.

Common questions

Minecraft FAQ

Should I buy Java or Bedrock?

On PC, buy the bundle and you get both. On console or mobile, Bedrock is your only — and correct — option.

Can Java and Bedrock players play together?

Not directly. Java players join Java servers; Bedrock players cross-play across console, mobile and PC.

Is Minecraft a one-time purchase?

Yes for the base game on each platform. Optional extras: Realms subscriptions, Marketplace content and server hosting.

Are cheap Minecraft keys safe?

Sometimes, but gray-market keys carry revocation risk. If the discount is dramatic, treat it as a red flag and buy official instead.
