How to Play Phantom Blade Zero — Combat, Weapons & Difficulty Guide

Kungfupunk combat, 30+ main weapons, four difficulty tiers, and beginner tips explained.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

Before You Start

Phantom Blade Zero is a third-person wuxia action RPG built around fast-paced swordplay and cinematic martial arts. The core isn't punishing Soulslike difficulty — it's "high-speed combat + deep buildcraft."

How to Play — Step-by-Step Guide

1
Choose a Difficulty

On first launch, pick: Wayfarer (cinematic, easy combat), Pathbreaker (balanced), Hellwalker (hard), or the 66 Days mode (permadeath). Beginners should pick Wayfarer.

2
Complete the Prologue Combat

The prologue teaches basic attacks, dodges, and parries. Follow the prompts to finish.

3
Learn Weapon Switching

Main weapons and Shadow Arsenal sub-weapons can be swapped anytime. Watch the weapon animations and cooldowns when switching.

4
Learn Weapon Reforging

Upgraded the wrong weapon? Don't worry. Find the reforge option to reclaim all materials and reinvest them in a new weapon.

5
Master the Sub-Weapon System

25 Shadow Arsenal weapons include great axes, bows, fire-breathing lion heads, and more. Some sub-weapons can fire at range.

6
Practice Dodging and Parrying

Enemies telegraph attacks. Dodge behind them for backstabs, or parry to counter.

7
Explore the Shadow Realm

Eight locations including Pang Town and the Illusion. Side quests can unlock new story paths and rare items.

8
Use the Summon System

Summon allies mid-combat, or take down enemies from afar. Especially useful in boss fights.

9
Manage the 66-Day Mechanic

The main story ties into the "66 days" concept. In some modes, dying consumes days — hit zero and it's permadeath.

10
Save Your Progress

The game supports manual saves. Save before boss fights to avoid repeating them.

Core Mechanics

Kungfupunk Combat

Traditional swordplay + high-speed martial arts + modern rock music. Combat uses health bars and a killer-intent gauge.

Weapon Reforging

Reclaim all materials from a wrongly upgraded weapon and reinvest. Experiment freely with different weapon combos.

Apex Attachments

Unlocked when a weapon hits max level. They grant unique effects without taking up regular attachment slots.

Shadow Arsenal Sub-Weapons

25 sub-weapons including great axes, bows, and fire-breathing lion heads. Some can fire at range.

Four Difficulty Tiers

Wayfarer, Pathbreaker, Hellwalker, and 66 Days mode. The 66 Days mode consumes life days on death.

Summon System

Summon allies in combat, or take down enemies from a distance.

Beginner Tips

  1. Start on Wayfarer to learn the story and combat rhythm first.
  2. Don't be afraid to switch weapons. The reforge system refunds all materials.
  3. Max out each weapon before deciding whether to reforge it — that maximizes material efficiency.
  4. Side quests unlock new story paths and rare items — don't just rush the main story.
  5. Save manually before boss fights to avoid repeating them.

FAQ

How do I play Phantom Blade Zero?

A third-person wuxia action RPG. You play as Soul, fighting and exploring the Shadow Realm. Combat centers on fast-paced swordplay and sub-weapon switching.

Is Phantom Blade Zero a Soulslike?

No. The developers have stated clearly that it's a wuxia action RPG, not a Soulslike.

How do I switch weapons in Phantom Blade Zero?

Main and sub-weapons can be swapped anytime. Wrongly upgraded weapons can be reforged to reclaim materials.

How many difficulty levels does Phantom Blade Zero have?

Four: Wayfarer, Pathbreaker, Hellwalker, and 66 Days mode.

Does Phantom Blade Zero have a tutorial?

Yes. The prologue walks you through basic attacks, dodges, and parries.

Does Phantom Blade Zero support Chinese?

Yes. It includes Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
