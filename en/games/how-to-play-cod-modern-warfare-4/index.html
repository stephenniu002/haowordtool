How to Play COD: Modern Warfare 4 — Controls, Maps & Loadouts

Controls, multiplayer maps, DMZ mode, Gunny loadouts, and beginner tips explained.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Advertisement

Before You Start

Modern Warfare 4 returns to grounded 6v6 gunplay with a new ballistics system — no random spread, every shot lands true. Launch includes three modes: Campaign, Multiplayer, and DMZ.

How to Play — Step-by-Step Guide

1
Complete the Training Course

First launch drops you into a training course covering basic movement, shooting, aiming, and throwing.

2
Pick a Mode

The main menu offers Campaign, Multiplayer, and DMZ. Beginners should start with the campaign to learn the feel.

3
Finish Campaign Chapter One

The campaign is set on the Korean Peninsula, where you play as young Korean soldier Park Hyun-joon. Chapter one teaches cover, peeking, and basic tactics.

4
Jump into Multiplayer

Pick Team Deathmatch or Domination to learn the maps. Launch includes 12 brand-new 6v6 maps.

5
Build Your Loadout

Open the Gunsmith to pick primary and secondary weapons. Use Gunny to one-tap switch between close-range, balanced, and long-range setups.

6
Test Your Weapons

Test new weapons at the firing range, compare attachments and performance, then head into matches.

7
Learn the Maps

Kill Block reconfigures its layout every round, and other maps have dynamic elements too. Play several rounds to learn the routes.

8
Try DMZ Mode

DMZ is set in the Khajin Exclusion Zone. Deploy solo or with a squad — the goal is to complete objectives and exfiltrate.

9
Unlock Apex Attachments

Max-level weapons unlock Apex attachments with unique effects that don't take up regular attachment slots.

10
Tune Your Settings

Adjust sensitivity, field of view, button layout, and aim assist to taste.

Core Mechanics

Ballistics System

Random spread is gone — hipfire is direct and predictable, matching exactly where the weapon points.

Gunny System

One-tap switching between close-range, balanced, and long-range weapon setups.

12 6v6 Maps

Launch includes 12 brand-new maps, with Kill Block reconfiguring its layout every round.

DMZ Extraction Mode

Set in the Khajin Exclusion Zone, one of the largest maps in series history. Deploy solo or with a squad.

Apex Attachments

Unlocked at max weapon level. Unique effects that don't take up regular attachment slots.

33 Launch Weapons

Covering assault rifles, SMGs, LMGs, sniper rifles, pistols, shotguns, and more.

Beginner Tips

  1. Play the campaign first to learn the feel, then head into multiplayer.
  2. Pick five favorite weapons and focus upgrades on them — don't try to max all 33.
  3. Use Gunny for fast loadout switching instead of rebuilding manually every time.
  4. Test every newly unlocked weapon at the firing range first.
  5. In close quarters, switching to your secondary is often faster than reloading.

FAQ

How do I play COD: Modern Warfare 4?

Launch includes three modes: Campaign, Multiplayer, and DMZ. The campaign is set on the Korean Peninsula, multiplayer returns to 6v6 ground combat, and DMZ is an extraction mode.

Does Modern Warfare 4 support Switch 2?

Yes. It's Call of Duty's first return to a Nintendo platform in 13 years.

How many difficulty levels does Modern Warfare 4 have?

The campaign supports multiple difficulties; multiplayer difficulty is set by matchmaking.

How do I build loadouts in Modern Warfare 4?

Build manually in the Gunsmith, or use Gunny to one-tap switch between close-range, balanced, and long-range setups.

Does Modern Warfare 4 have a tutorial?

Yes. First launch includes a training course, and campaign chapter one has teaching segments.

Can I play Modern Warfare 4 offline?

The campaign can be played offline. Multiplayer and DMZ require an internet connection.
