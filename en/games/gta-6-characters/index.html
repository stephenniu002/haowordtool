Dual Protagonists

GTA 6 is the series' first dual-protagonist open-world game. Players can switch between Jason and Lucia at any time — each with independent stats, skills, and equipment.

🤠 Jason Duval

One of GTA 6's two protagonists and Lucia's partner. Switch to Jason during free roam to experience his exclusive missions and storylines.

👩‍🦰 Lucia Caminos

One of GTA 6's two protagonists — the series' first female lead. Partners with Jason, with independent stats, skills, and equipment; some missions can only be triggered by Lucia.

Dual-Protagonist System

🔄 Character Switching

During free roam, hold the down D-pad button (or hold Tab) to open the character switch menu and pick Jason or Lucia. Missions usually lock the current character.

📊 Independent Stats

Jason and Lucia each have independent stats, skills, and equipment. Build them separately — nothing is shared.

🎯 Exclusive Missions

Some missions can only be triggered by a specific protagonist. Switch regularly to check available missions so you don't miss story branches.

💞 Relationship

Side activities and random events affect the relationship between the two characters and later story options. Gym sessions, pool, restaurants, and rideshares all shape their bond.

Character Building Tips

  • Build both evenly: Don't focus on just one character — train both protagonists to avoid one falling behind late-game.
  • Switch regularly: Check available missions after switching; some missions lock the current character.
  • Mind the relationship: Side choices affect their relationship, which influences later story options.
