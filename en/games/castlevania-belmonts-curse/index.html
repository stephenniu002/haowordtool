Protagonist & Story

The protagonist, Rose Belmont, is the daughter of Trevor Belmont. Set in Paris, 1499 — 23 years after the events of Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse — Rose descends into the Paris catacombs and the dark corridors of Dracula's Castle, wielding the sacred whip "Vampire Killer" plus an arsenal of weapons and skills against the monsters within.

This is the first mainline 2D Castlevania in 18 years. The Metroidvania genre name itself comes from this series.

Core Gameplay

Weapon System

  • Main weapons: 30+ types, including whips, swords, and war hammers
  • Sub-weapons ("Shadow Arsenal"): 25+ types, including throwing axes, bows, and a fire-breathing lion head
  • Upgrades: the "Reverse Refining Talisman" resets upgrades and refunds all materials — no wasted resources

Combat Mechanics

  • Parry & dodge: parry when enemies flash blue; use "Ghost Step" to slip behind enemies for backstabs
  • "Killing Intent" combos: specific button sequences break enemy guards fast
  • Three whip uses: attack, grapple, and swing — for combat and exploration
  • Holy Light Block: the core defensive mechanic — learn it first

Difficulty Options

  • Hellwalker Mode: smarter, adaptive enemy AI
  • 66 Days Mode: higher-challenge difficulty

Beginner Tips

  1. Master Holy Light Block first — it's the most important defensive mechanic in the demo. Practice in a safe area before pushing the main story.
  2. Use the whip's grapple & swing to find routes — the whip isn't just a weapon, it's an exploration tool. Grappling and swinging reach platforms normal jumps can't.
  3. Equip up to 3 relics at once — relics grant passive bonuses; you can equip up to 3. Long-press to instantly equip a newly picked-up weapon.
  4. Collect Hero's Heart & Sage's Heart fragments — several collectible fragments exist in the demo; plan your route to grab them within the 20-minute limit.
  5. Use the Reverse Refining Talisman — never fear wasting upgrade resources. The talisman resets upgraded weapons and refunds all materials.

FAQ

When does Castlevania: Belmont's Curse release?

October 15, 2026 for consoles; the Steam version unlocks October 14.

Does Castlevania: Belmont's Curse support Chinese?

Yes. The game includes Simplified and Traditional Chinese, plus Japanese, Korean, and English subtitles.

Is Castlevania: Belmont's Curse a Metroidvania?

Yes. It's a 2D exploration-focused action game in the Metroidvania genre — and the first mainline 2D Castlevania in 18 years.

Who is the protagonist of Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?

Rose Belmont, daughter of Trevor Belmont. The story is set in Paris, 1499.

What difficulty modes does Castlevania: Belmont's Curse have?

Standard mode, Hellwalker mode (smarter AI), and 66 Days Mode (higher challenge).

Is there a demo for Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?

Yes. A demo launched October 1, 2026, covering the game's opening — saves carry over to the full release.
