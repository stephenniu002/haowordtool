How to Make a Sprunki Mod on Scratch — Beginner Tutorial

Build your own Sprunki-style music game on Scratch, step by step. No prior coding experience needed.

Step-by-Step Tutorial

1
Create a Scratch account

Go to scratch.mit.edu, sign up free, and verify your email so you can publish projects.

2
Start from a remixable template

Search Scratch for a Sprunki or Incredibox-style template marked remixable. Remixing gives you the drag-and-drop stage logic for free.

3
Design your characters

Replace the template sprites with your own characters. Keep them simple — bold shapes read best at small sizes. Name each sprite after its sound role (beat, melody, vocal, effect).

4
Add your sounds

Record or import one loop per character. Trim each loop to the same length (e.g. 4 bars) so layers stay in sync — this is the most important technical step.

5
Wire sounds to characters

In each character sprite, add a when-this-sprite-clicked/placed script that plays its loop. Copy the pattern from the template.

6
Build the stage logic

Make sure placing a character starts its loop and removing it stops the loop. Test with 3–4 characters layered.

7
Add a horror trigger (optional)

Add a Black Hat-style sprite that, when placed, swaps every character to a dark costume and switches loops to eerie versions — this is what makes Sprunki mods go viral.

8
Test everything

Playtest: all loops in sync, mute/remove works, no overlapping audio glitches. Fix timing issues before publishing.

9
Publish and credit

Share the project, write a clear description, and credit the template creator and any assets you remixed.

Tips for a Great Sprunki Mod

  • Sync is everything: loops of mismatched lengths are the #1 beginner mistake. Keep every loop the same bar count and tempo.
  • Limit your first roster: 6–8 characters is plenty for v1. You can always expand later.
  • Give each character a distinct role: don't make five characters that all sound like drums.
  • The horror trigger is your viral hook: even a simple costume swap doubles shareability.
  • Credit your sources: the Sprunki community is strict about credit — always name template and asset creators.

FAQ

Can beginners make a Sprunki mod on Scratch?

Yes. Start by remixing an existing Sprunki-style template — you get the drag-and-drop logic for free and only need to swap art and sounds.

Do I need to know how to code?

Basic Scratch block coding is enough. A remixable template handles the hard parts; you mainly edit sprites and sounds.

How long does it take to make a Sprunki mod?

A first playable version takes about 2–4 hours. Polished mods with horror modes take longer.

Can I use other people's Sprunki art in my mod?

Only with permission and credit. The community takes asset credit seriously — draw your own or use explicitly remixable assets.

How do I publish my Sprunki mod?

Click Share on your Scratch project page after verifying your email. Write a clear description and credit everyone.
