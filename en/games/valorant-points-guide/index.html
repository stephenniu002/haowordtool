A quick orientation

VP is region-locked. Buy it right the first time.

Valorant Points (VP) are Valorant's premium currency, spent on weapon skins, agents, and the battle pass. They are purchased with real money inside the Riot client or via official gift cards.

The key detail: VP and your account region are linked. Points bought in one region generally cannot move to another, so always buy for the region your account actually plays in.

Region warning: If you created your Riot account while traveling or with a VPN, your store region may surprise you. Check the region shown in the client store before buying VP or gift cards.

Common questions

Valorant FAQ

Can I transfer VP to another region?

Generally no. VP is tied to your account's region. Contact Riot support if you believe your region is set incorrectly.

Are third-party VP sellers safe?

No. Unofficial VP sellers risk revoked points and account penalties. Use the in-client store or authorized gift cards.

Do Valorant Points expire?

No. VP stays in your account until you spend it.

What's the difference between VP and Radianite?

VP is bought with real money for skins and passes. Radianite upgrades skin variants and comes from the battle pass or separate purchase.

No. This is an independent informational guide. It does not sell VP or collect account credentials.
