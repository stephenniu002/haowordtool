Websites & landing pages
How to choose well.

Compare templates, do-it-yourself tools and custom builds for your stage of business.

HaoWord Studio · Editorial guide · Updated 2026-09-14

Choose one primary outcome

A service brochure, an enquiry page and an online store require different information and functions. Define what a visitor should do, then list what they need to make that decision. A focused service page is easier to scope than a site full of unprepared features.

Compare templates, builders and custom work

Templates suit projects with ready copy and images and mostly presentational changes. A website builder suits someone who wants ongoing control within platform limits. Custom work suits defined workflows or integrations. If the brief is unclear, prototype before requesting a final quote.

Clarify ownership and recurring costs

Ask who owns the domain, hosting, source code and admin accounts. Confirm export options and paid plugin dependencies. Specify who handles updates, backups and maintenance. Separate setup fees from ongoing costs so you can compare the total commitment.

Accept the work through a real user journey

Complete the primary task on mobile and desktop. Check form delivery, link destinations, errors and final copy. Login, payments, email and storage require working services behind the page. A visible button alone does not prove that an integration is complete.
