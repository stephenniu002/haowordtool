Recommendations & affiliate disclosure

What these guides provide

We publish service selection guides, not claims that we have hired or tested every provider. Third-party prices, availability and scope are determined at purchase. When affiliate links are configured, eligible purchases may earn us a commission.

How affiliate links are disclosed

Links with a commission relationship are labelled as affiliate links next to the button. Links without an affiliate configuration are labelled as standard platform links. We do not promise that every transaction earns a commission or that a purchase guarantees a particular business result.

How to make a choice

Our guides focus on fit, relevant portfolios, scope, revisions and usage rights. This section currently provides category guides and platform browsing links. We do not publish unverified seller ratings, sales counts, discounts or claims of firsthand testing.

Who delivers the service

Third-party services are delivered by their providers. Pricing, payment, refunds and disputes follow the terms applicable to the transaction. HaoWord’s own templates, tools and custom services link to their separate pages.

How HaoWordTool makes money

HaoWordTool earns revenue in three ways. First, affiliate commissions: when you buy through certain links on this site, the seller pays us a commission. This never raises the price you pay — the commission comes out of the seller's margin, and in some cases our links carry the same or better pricing than going direct. Second, our own store: we sell selected physical products through our Shopify store, where we are the merchant and set the price, shipping terms, and return policy ourselves. Third, services: our AI Studio offers product photography, video, and voiceover packages, and we publish paid courses and skill packs.

Knowing this matters because it tells you where our incentives sit. Affiliate revenue rewards us for recommending things people actually buy and keep. That is why our guides emphasise fit and trade-offs over hype: a reader who buys the wrong product on our recommendation and returns it earns us nothing and costs us trust.

Editorial independence

Rankings and recommendations on this site are not for sale. No seller can pay for a placement, a higher rank, or the removal of a criticism. When we compare options, the order reflects our assessment of fit for the reader's stated need — features, limitations, price, and terms — not who pays us the most.

When we have not personally tested or hired something, we say so. Our service guides are selection guides: they teach you how to brief, compare, and choose providers using verifiable criteria such as portfolios, revision terms, and usage rights. We do not publish star ratings, sales counts, or "tested by us" claims for providers we have not worked with. If a guide is based on hands-on experience, it says what was tested and when.

How affiliate links are labelled

Every link with a commission relationship is labelled as an affiliate link next to the button or link text, and outbound affiliate links carry the appropriate sponsored attributes in the page code. Links without an affiliate arrangement are labelled as standard platform links. We review these labels when pages are updated, but configurations change — if a label looks wrong, tell us and we will fix it.

Price and availability accuracy

Prices, discounts, stock levels, and promotion terms change constantly and are controlled by the seller, not by us. Treat every price on this site as a snapshot from the time of writing: always confirm the current price, shipping cost, and return terms on the seller's own page before you buy. Our buying guides carry last-updated dates, and we prioritise refreshing pages where prices move frequently, but we cannot guarantee real-time accuracy on third-party listings.
