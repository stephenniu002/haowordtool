Video editing & captions
How to choose well.

Define the footage, editing scope, caption languages and export formats in one brief.

HaoWord Studio · Editorial guide · Updated 2026-09-14

Start with the footage and destination

Count the raw footage duration, resolution and audio condition. Specify the publishing platform, aspect ratio, language and final runtime. A one-minute finished video can require very different effort depending on how much source material must be reviewed.

Separate editing from additional production

Editing, noise reduction, colour work, captions, translation, voiceover and motion graphics are separate tasks. List what you need and check whether bilingual captions include translation and proofreading. A text-only brief requiring all-new visuals is a production job, not just an edit.

Agree on a sample and revision process

Share a reference and explain whether you mean its pacing, captions or structure. Consider agreeing on a short sample before the full edit. Define revision rounds, feedback deadlines and the cost of replacing an approved script. Distinguish factual corrections from changes in creative direction.

Check delivery and usage terms

Watch on a phone to check caption readability, names, numbers and voice clarity. Confirm aspect ratios, encoding, caption files and editable project files. Ask about music, footage and voice rights: permission on one platform does not automatically cover every commercial channel.
