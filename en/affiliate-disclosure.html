Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on haowordtool.com are affiliate links. Here is exactly what that means for you.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a special URL that lets a merchant know we referred you. If you click that link and make a purchase, the merchant may pay us a commission. The price you pay is exactly the same — you are not charged anything extra.

On this site, affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored" in the page code, and pages that contain them carry a visible disclosure notice.

Programs we participate in

We currently participate in affiliate programs through third-party affiliate networks, including but not limited to:

  • MyLead — including offers for game top-up services such as Codashop (game credits and diamonds for titles like Free Fire MAX)
  • Other affiliate networks we may join from time to time for relevant gaming, software, or tool offers

When we add a new program, this page will be updated.

Does it cost you more?

No. Affiliate commissions are paid by the merchant out of their own margin. You pay the same price you would pay by visiting the merchant directly. In some cases, affiliate offers may even include exclusive discounts.

How we choose what to recommend

We only link to services we believe are useful to our readers — for example, official or widely used top-up stores for games we already cover in our guides. A commission never changes our editorial judgment: if a service is unreliable or unsafe, we will not recommend it regardless of commission.

How tracking works

When you click an affiliate link, the affiliate network may set a cookie or use a similar identifier to attribute a later purchase to our referral. This is standard practice for affiliate programs. You can block these cookies in your browser settings, but doing so means we will not receive credit for the referral. See our Cookie Policy for details on how to manage cookies.

Support our free content

HaoWordTool's guides and tools are free to use. Affiliate commissions are one of the ways we keep them free — they help cover hosting, content creation, and maintenance costs. Thank you for supporting us when you shop through our links.
