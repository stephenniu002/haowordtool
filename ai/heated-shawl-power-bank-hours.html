How Many Hours Will a 10000mAh Power Bank
Run a Heated Shawl?

Listings claim anywhere from 4 to 10 hours, and they can’t all be right. Here is the actual math — and the honest reasons your real-world number will differ.

By the HaoWordTool team Last updated: October 2026 5 min read
Disclosure: we sell our own heated shawl ($81.99, USB 5V/2A, 10W) and it does not include a power bank — you bring your own. This guide is written for the power bank already sitting in your drawer, not to sell you a new one.

1The short answer: about 3 hours on high

The math is simple once you convert everything to the same unit — watt-hours (Wh):

10000mAh × 3.7V = 37Wh (rated energy)
37Wh × ~85% conversion efficiency ≈ 31.5Wh (usable energy)
31.5Wh ÷ 10W (high heat) ≈ 3.1 hours

So a standard 10000mAh bank gives the HaoWordTool heated shawl roughly 3 hours on high. Medium and low draw less power, so runtime stretches — roughly 4–5 hours and 6–8 hours. Those two are estimates: no brand publishes per-level wattage, so exact decimals for medium and low would be guesswork.

2Why the “10000mAh” number is misleading

Here is the detail most listings skip: a power bank’s mAh rating is measured at the internal cell voltage of 3.7V, not at the 5V coming out of the USB port. Boosting 3.7V up to 5V loses energy, and the conversion circuitry loses a bit more. In practice you get roughly 85% of the rated energy as usable 5V output.

This is also why two “10000mAh” banks can behave differently. Cheap banks with inflated capacity claims or tired old cells deliver far less than the label promises. If your shawl dies after 90 minutes on a bargain-bin bank, the bank is the suspect — not the shawl.

  • Check the output rating printed on your bank: you need a port labeled 5V/2A (or higher). A 5V/1A port will underpower a 10W shawl.
  • Use a decent cable. A thin, damaged, or over-long cable drops voltage and wastes energy as heat in the cable itself.
  • 10000mAh is the sweet spot for most people: enough for an evening on medium, light enough to forget it’s in your pocket.

3The honest variables: why your mileage will differ

Anyone promising an exact runtime is selling certainty they don’t have. Four things move the number:

  • Ambient temperature. Lithium batteries perform worse in the cold — the same bank lasts noticeably shorter on a freezing porch than in a warm living room.
  • Heat level. High draws the full 10W continuously. Lower levels cycle or draw less — this is the single biggest lever on runtime.
  • Bank honesty. Reputable brands deliver close to their rated capacity. No-name banks with fantasy numbers do not. The shawl can only use what the bank actually holds.
  • Bank age. A two-year-old bank that lived in a hot car has less usable capacity than its label says. If runtime suddenly drops, try a fresher bank before blaming the shawl.

4Why heated shawls don’t ship with a power bank

Fair question, and the answer isn’t that brands are cheap. Lithium batteries are a headache to ship. Airlines restrict them, they cost significantly more to move as hazardous goods than as part of a normal parcel, and a punctured or faulty cell in transit is a genuine fire risk. For a small heated-apparel brand, putting a battery in every box means slower shipping, higher freight bills, and real safety liability.

So the industry settled on the sensible default: sell the garment, let you power it with a bank you already own. Nearly every USB heated shawl, vest, and scarf on the market works this way — it’s standard practice, not a corner being cut. It also avoids e-waste: most households already have one or two power banks in a drawer, and shipping you a third one helps nobody.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a 10000mAh power bank power a heated shawl?

About 3 hours on high (10W). Roughly 4–5 hours on medium and 6–8 hours on low — those are estimates, since per-level wattage isn’t published by any brand we know of.

Does the HaoWordTool heated shawl come with a power bank?

No. You supply your own bank with a 5V-2A USB output. This is industry-standard: lithium batteries are restricted and expensive to ship by air, so heated apparel brands don’t bundle them.

Why is real runtime shorter than the mAh number suggests?

Capacity is rated at the 3.7V cell voltage, not the 5V USB output. After boosting to 5V and conversion losses, roughly 85% of the rated energy is actually usable.

Can I use a 20000mAh bank for longer runtime?

Yes — it roughly doubles runtime (about 6 hours on high). The trade-off is weight: a 20000mAh bank is noticeably heavier in a pocket.

Does cold weather affect how long the bank lasts?

Yes — lithium batteries deliver less energy in the cold. Keep the bank in an inside pocket, close to your body.
