📈 Spot the wave half a year early

AI Trends Board

10 rising AI directions. Each gets three lines: why it's rising, how to enter, where the risk is. Trends are for positioning early, not for chasing.

#1

AI Coding Agents

From "code completion" to "shipping features solo" — Cursor, Claude Code and Kimi Code are turning programmers into supervisors.

Why it's rising: reasoning models + long context let agents complete multi-step engineering tasks alone; companies pay for "fewer headcount".How to enter: build agents for vertical scenarios (testing, migration, docs) — don't build a general IDE.Risks: giants are entering directly; the window for indie tools is short.
#2

AI Video Generation

After Veo 3, Sora and Kling, Nano Banana-class image editing just lowered the bar another notch.

Why it's rising: short video is the biggest content pool; AI cut production cost from thousands of dollars to a few bucks.How to enter: build vertical templates (e-commerce product videos, wedding shorts); sell the result, not the tool.Risks: models iterate fast; tool differentiation is hard to sustain.
#3

AI Companionship

Character.ai proved session length, Replika proved willingness to pay — emotion is the most expensive commodity.

Why it's rising: loneliness is a real need; real-time voice interaction transforms the companionship experience.How to enter: serve niche audiences (elderly companionship, kids' story machines); stay clear of gray areas.Risks: compliance is a minefield; minor protection is make-or-break.
#4

AI Search / Answer Engines

After Perplexity, Google AI Mode rolled out broadly — the search paradigm is being rewritten.

Why it's rising: users want answers, not links; traditional SEO traffic is being eaten by AI summaries.How to enter: build vertical answer engines (legal/medical/code); cite sources to build trust.Risks: Google itself is your biggest competitor.
#5

Open-Source Local Deployment

DeepSeek R1, Kimi K2, Llama 4 — each generation stronger; running big models locally is now real.

Why it's rising: privacy compliance + cost; governments, enterprises and global teams need "their own model".How to enter: sell deployment services/private solutions; sell the service, not the model.Risks: service-heavy and hard to scale; solo founders think twice.
#6

AI Workflow Automation

n8n-style workflows + AI nodes give small teams big-company execution power.

Why it's rising: companies pay far more readily for "saving headcount" than for "cool".How to enter: build industry template packs (e-commerce/law firms/clinics); templates are the acquisition.Risks: you need to understand the business; pure tech backgrounds struggle to land it.
#7

AI Real-Time Voice

After GPT-4o-class real-time voice, phone support, speaking coaches and voice companions all get rebuilt.

Why it's rising: latency dropped to human-conversation level; voice becomes the new interaction entry point.How to enter: build for phone scenarios (booking/follow-up/outbound); charge on outcomes.Risks: do the math on call costs and compliance (recording consent).
#8

AI Image Editing

Nano Banana made "edit this image in one sentence" everyday; retouching moved from pro software to a chat box.

Why it's rising: API prices halved; editing cost approaches zero; scenarios are exploding.How to enter: build for e-commerce (swap backgrounds/models/bulk generation) — closest to the money.Risks: fast commoditization; first-mover advantage lasts only months.
#9

AI Education / Tutoring

After photo-solve came full-subject tutors; parents never hesitate to pay for "my kid improving".

Why it's rising: multimodal + long context lets AI actually "teach", not just hand out answers.How to enter: nail one subject (math/English speaking); word of mouth spreads in parent groups.Risks: education compliance differs by region — check policy first.
#10

AI E-commerce Content

Product photos, listing pages and sales videos are all going AI; e-commerce content production is being rebuilt.

Why it's rising: merchants want "orders"; AI pushed content cost to the floor.How to enter: run agency-style services and take a cut of results — more lucrative than selling tools.Risks: platform rules shift fast (AI-content labeling); keep a backup plan.
How to use this board: pick 1 industry you know + 1 trend, and work the intersection. Trend × industry = opportunity; trends alone are correct-but-useless platitudes.

Honest note: this board is the editor's synthesis of public information and judgment — no forecast data, not investment advice. Updated monthly.
