Worked example

Ten working days from Monday 26 October 2026 lands on Monday 9 November, crossing two weekends — 14 calendar days for 10 working ones.

Working-day arithmetic is what contracts use: "within 10 business days" is a different deadline from "within 10 days", and the difference is usually a weekend and a holiday.

Holidays are yours to supply

There is no universal holiday list. List the dates that apply to your jurisdiction and your year, one per line, and they are skipped as well as weekends. Without them the result is a weekdays-only count.

Does the clock start on day one

Most legal definitions exclude the day of the triggering event: a notice served on Monday with a five-business-day period expires the following Monday, not the Friday. That single convention moves every deadline by a day, and it is the most common source of "we were on time" disputes. When the period is expressed in weeks rather than days, the rule is usually that it ends on the same weekday as it began.

If the last day falls on a weekend or public holiday, deadlines normally roll to the next working day. Which holidays apply depends on where the recipient is, not where the sender is — another reason to generate the date rather than count by hand.

Holidays are regional, not national. A country with several public holiday calendars can have a bank holiday in one part and a working day in another, and companies sometimes follow a different calendar again. For anything contractual, name the calendar you are counting against — the difference is usually a single day, which is exactly enough to miss a filing window.
