What Is a Line Break?

A line break is an invisible character that moves text to the next line. There are two kinds: a manual line break (created with Shift+Enter), which stays inside the same paragraph, and a paragraph break (created with Enter), which starts a brand-new paragraph. When you copy text from PDFs, websites or spreadsheets, these breaks often land in the wrong places — chopping sentences in half or adding double spacing.

This tool strips those unwanted breaks and formatting artifacts in one click, turning messy copied text into clean, flowing paragraphs.

When to Remove Line Breaks

Cleaning PDF Copy-Paste Text

Text copied from PDFs usually breaks at the end of every visual line, not at sentence boundaries. Removing line breaks (with "replace with space" on) reassembles the original paragraphs instantly.

Preparing Text for SEO

Meta descriptions, title tags and alt text must be single-line. Stray line breaks from a spreadsheet or doc can break your markup or get stripped unpredictably by the CMS.

Formatting Data for Spreadsheets

Line breaks inside spreadsheet cells cause display and import problems. Clean the text first, then paste it into Excel or Google Sheets as clean single-line values.

Fixing Emails and Documents

Forwarded emails and chat exports often contain hard-wrapped lines. Removing the breaks restores readable paragraphs you can quote or archive.

FAQ

How do I remove line breaks in Word?

In Word, press Ctrl+H to open Find and Replace. Enter ^p in "Find what" to remove paragraph marks, or ^l to remove manual line breaks (Shift+Enter), and replace with a space.

How do I remove line breaks in Excel?

Use Find and Replace (Ctrl+H), enter Ctrl+J in "Find what", and replace with a space. Or use the formula =SUBSTITUTE(SUBSTITUTE(A1,CHAR(10)," "),CHAR(13)," ").

How do I remove line breaks in Google Docs?

Open Find and Replace (Ctrl+H), check "Match using regular expressions", enter \n in "Find" and a space in "Replace with", then click Replace all.

What is the difference between a line break and a paragraph break?

A line break (Shift+Enter) moves text to the next line within the same paragraph. A paragraph break (Enter) ends the paragraph and starts a new one with paragraph spacing.
