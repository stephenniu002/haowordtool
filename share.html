Suggested classroom uses

  • Explore possible words from a fixed set of letters.
  • Compare word length, meaning, pronunciation and game score.
  • Practice spelling patterns such as five-letter words and words ending in -ing.
  • Discuss why a candidate word is valid instead of copying an answer without reflection.
