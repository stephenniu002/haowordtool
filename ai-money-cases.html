The 8 Rules — How AI Actually Makes Money

Read this first. The 30 cases below are just evidence for these rules. When you're stuck, come back here.

RULE 01

Never sell AI. Sell a human desire.

Money, trust, less hassle, companionship, curiosity, progress. AI only makes delivery cheaper. If you can't name the desire, don't build it.

RULE 02

Growth = users spreading for you.

Make usage itself shareable: screenshots, reactions, creations. The best ad is a user who can't help posting.

RULE 03

Find demand 3 ways.

Your own pain. Other people's repeated pleas ("how do I reply to this?"). Comment mining — turn a user's exact sentence into the product.

RULE 04

The formula.

Peak-pain moment → AI removes the hassle → add a hook (gamification / emotion / infinite content) → usage becomes the ad.

RULE 05

The 3 questions (Base44 test).

Do users get results fast? Is acquisition light? Do costs scale down with volume? Three yeses before you go all in.

RULE 06

Free economics.

97% free users are your sales team; 3% payers are your profit. Set free limits on day one — they're a cost firewall, not generosity.

RULE 07

Price on pain removed.

Anchor pricing to the hassle or time you save the user — never to your cost. Price for who actually pays.

RULE 08

Divide headlines by 5.

Case-study revenue numbers are unverified. Read the logic, not the digits.

Each card: what it does, reported revenue, how it wins, and the one lesson to steal.

#01Aggregator

$30K/mo MRR*

Aggregates free-stuff listings. Built fast with AI, promoted through creator videos.

How it wins: sells "certainty of saving money" — the listings are free everywhere, reliability is the product.
Steal this: Sell certainty, not information.
#02Gamified tool

How it wins: sells the mechanism that makes you do it, not posture knowledge everyone already has.
Steal this: Don't sell knowledge. Sell "getting it done."
#03Trust

Verifies income screenshots. Built with Cursor by Mark.

How it wins: turned "distrust" into a traffic entrance; monetizes via ad slots + 3% deal commission.
Steal this: Trust itself is a business.
#04Parenting

€47.99/yr subscription

#05Emotion

$99/yr · $7M+ post-Shark-Tank*

How it wins: shrank "write a book for mom" into "answer one question a week." The QR + real voice sells fighting forgetting.

#06Viral consumer

Upload a pet photo, pick a song — the pet sings it.

#26Utility

$50K / 28 days*

To dismiss the alarm: put the phone down, do push-ups on camera. Verified, then silence.

#27Creative AI

#28Dev tool · $80M exit

"Build me a CRM" → pages, database, auth, deployment. Solo founder, zero funding, 6 months to exit.

How it wins — the 3-layer flywheel: (1) fast delivery — sell results, not tools; (2) light acquisition — users' shared apps are the ads; (3) low cost — own model (Base1) took margins from ~0% toward 60%.

How it wins: AI products are evolving from "give answers" to "show the work." Process = trust. When everyone can generate, taste is the moat.
Steal this: In the AI era, compete on taste, not compute.
#30Design AI
