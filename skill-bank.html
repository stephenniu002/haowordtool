Before you choose

What is included?

Each kit contains an English SKILL.md, a request template, a worked decision example, a quality rubric, three evaluation scenarios, research sources and a product cover. The ZIP also includes a Chinese reference note.

How do I use a skill?

Provide your material and the instructions to an AI assistant that reads Markdown. Hosts supporting SKILL.md can load the entire skill folder through their documented installation process. AI subscriptions, API credits and third-party software are not included.

How is my purchase delivered?

Pay for one skill through PayPal, then submit the delivery request with your transaction ID, product and email. HaoWordTool verifies the payment and sends the matching file or private download link. Delivery is manually verified, not an instant automatic download.

What do the GitHub references mean?

The collection was researched against 20 recently updated repositories with more than 5,000 stars in the September 27, 2026 snapshot. These are independently written workflows. No repository code, logos or skill text are bundled, and the original maintainers do not endorse these products. Stars belong to the reference repositories.

What has been checked?

File structure, package integrity and cover layouts have been checked. The evaluation scenarios are included for your own testing; the skills have not been benchmarked across every model or host. Results depend on your inputs and AI tools.
