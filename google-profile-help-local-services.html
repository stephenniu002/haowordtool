Your Reviews Don't Match Across the Web — And It's Costing You Clients

Nobody hires a dentist, a lawyer, or a contractor from a single page. They cross-check: Google, Yelp, industry directories. When the numbers don't agree, they assume the worst one is the truth — and call whoever looks consistent.

The three most common problems we see

  • Rating gaps across directories. A 4.8 on Google next to a 2.8 on a major industry directory doesn't average out in a client's mind — the low number wins. Bringing platforms in line is usually the fastest trust repair there is.
  • Thin or outdated profile. Missing services, no photos of the office or team, vague descriptions. High-ticket clients won't book a consultation with a business they can't see.
  • Unanswered reviews. A negative review with no response reads as "they don't care." For a service business, one calm, professional reply can be worth more than a dozen five-star ratings.

What a profile optimization service does

It isn't magic, and it isn't fake reviews. A legitimate optimization service does the unglamorous work most owners never get around to:

  • Completing every field: services, hours, categories, attributes, service areas.
  • Adding current photos of the office, the team, and the work itself.
  • Writing professional responses to existing reviews — including the bad ones.
  • Setting up a routine so new reviews get answered instead of piling up.
  • Cleaning up inconsistent names, addresses, and phone numbers across directories.
