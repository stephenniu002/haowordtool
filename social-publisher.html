How it works

  1. Add: Choose a platform above and open the connection page.
  2. Authorize: Log in on the platform and approve the connection. Complete any verification there.
  3. Publish: Once status shows Connected, videos can be published or scheduled to that account.

This page is an overview. Publishing itself is handled by the publishing dashboard / API after the account is connected.
