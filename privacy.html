Privacy Policy

The solver runs locally. This policy explains the limited data that hosting, advertising, and direct contact can involve.

Our hosting provider may process IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamp, and security logs to deliver and protect the site. We do not receive your browser passwords or unrelated browsing history.

We use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted. Consent choices may be offered based on location. You can review Google’s advertising controls and your browser’s cookie settings.

Email

If you contact us, we receive the address and content you choose to send. We use it to respond, investigate corrections, and maintain reasonable records. Do not email sensitive identity or payment information.

Orders and digital delivery

When you request a product or service, we may receive your name, email address, country, requested product and the information needed to confirm delivery. For a Google Drive course, the Google email you supply is used to grant viewer access. For a blockchain payment, we may record the public transaction hash, asset, network, amount and status. Never send a private key, seed phrase, account password or full payment-card number.

Payment and file-service providers

If third-party checkout is enabled, that provider processes payment and receipt data under its own privacy terms. Google processes Drive access information when a course is shared. We receive only the transaction and delivery information needed to confirm the order, provide access, handle support and keep required business records.

Retention and sharing

We do not sell contact information. Service providers may process data to host, secure, measure, or advertise the site under their own terms. We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for support, legal, or security purposes.

Your choices

You may block cookies, use browser privacy controls, or contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of email correspondence, subject to legal and security limits.
