Phantom Blade Zero Guide

Release date: October 29, 2026 (PC via Steam). A wuxia action RPG on Unreal Engine 5 from S-GAME Studio.

What Is It?

You play as Soul, an assassin with 66 days left to live, fighting through a conspiracy in a stylized wuxia world. Fast, combo-heavy combat with parries and weapon switching. It topped Steam's most-wishlisted chart for October 2026.

Combat Basics

  • Learn parry timing early — it opens enemies up for big damage.
  • Switch weapons mid-combo to keep pressure on bosses.
  • Save healing for phase transitions, not chip damage.

Before You Buy

  • PC (Steam) first; console versions unconfirmed.
  • Single-player, story-driven — no live-service grind.
  • Check the PC requirements on Steam before pre-ordering.
