Sprunki Guide — How to Play, Characters, Phases & Mods

What Is Sprunki?

Sprunki is a free fan-made browser music game. Drag colorful characters onto a stage — each one plays a different sound (beats, melodies, vocals, effects) — and mix them into your own track. No download, no signup, just open and play.

How to Play

  1. Pick characters: Choose from the lineup at the bottom. Each has a unique sound and animation.
  2. Drag onto the stage: Place up to 7 characters to build your mix.
  3. Control sounds: Click the icons under a character to mute, solo, or remove them.
  4. Experiment: Certain combinations unlock secret animations and horror-themed phases.
  5. Share: Save your mix and share it with the community.

Characters

Each Sprunki character brings a distinct sound role — drummers keep the beat, singers carry melody, and effect characters add texture. Try muting the drums to hear the melody layer on its own.

Phases

The community has built multiple phases, from the cheerful original to horror-themed variants. Phase 1–5 each add new characters, sounds and visuals. Unlock hidden phases by trying unusual character combinations.

Popular Mods

  • Sprunki Retake — polished remake with new visuals
  • Wenda Treatment — fan-favorite horror mod
  • Sprunki Phase 3 / Phase 4 — expanded sound libraries

Tips

  • Start with 2–3 characters, then layer up — too many at once sounds muddy.
  • Use solo mode to hear what each character contributes.
  • Horror phases hide easter eggs — try the black-hat character last.
