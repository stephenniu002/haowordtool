EDITORIAL POLICY · 编辑政策

我们对内容的要求，和我们对自己的要求。

中文在前，英文在后。Chinese first, English below.

内容标准

  • 原创。每篇文章都有自己的实测过程、数据或分析，不直接复制他人内容，也不做语序调换式的洗稿。
  • 有用。读者看完能动手做出结果：工具页打开即用，教程页按步骤能复现，榜单页能看懂排名怎么来的。
  • 不洗稿。引用别人的观点会注明出处；不同意的会说明理由，不冒充。

AI 使用披露

本站使用 AI 的方式如实如下：

  • 会用的环节：选题研究时做信息搜集整理；初稿写作时辅助起草；跨语言版本时辅助翻译与润色。
  • 不会用的环节：实测数据和榜单分数不由 AI 编造；结论不由 AI 代下；最终发布不经人工不开。
  • 人工审核：每一篇内容发布前都经过 Stephen Liu 本人核验——核对事实、复跑示例、检查数据来源，确认无误后才上线。未经人工核验的 AI 输出不会发布。

事实核查流程

  1. 工具与教程类：示例在当前公开环境中实际运行一遍，记录可复现的结果；注明测试环境与局限。
  2. 榜单类：评分维度、数据来源、采集时间、更新周期随榜单一起公布；任何人用同样的公开数据能复算出同样的排名。
  3. 引用类：转述的第三方数据给出原始出处链接；找不到原始出处的说法不写进正文。
  4. 商业隔离：广告和商业合作不参与内容评审，不改变教程结论和榜单排名。

更正机制

  • 我会先复现/核验问题，确认属实后再修改页面；
  • 每次更正都会在页面上标注更正日期与更正内容，不会悄悄改掉；
  • 事实类更正目标在 7 个工作日内处理完毕。

Content standards

  • Original. Every article carries its own testing, data or analysis. We do not copy other people's work, and we do not paraphrase-spin it either.
  • Useful. A reader should be able to act on it: tools work on open, tutorials reproduce step by step, rankings explain how they were computed.
  • No rewriting theft. We cite the source of ideas we borrow; when we disagree, we say why — never passing it off as our own.

AI usage disclosure

Here is exactly how this site uses AI:

  • Where AI is used: gathering and organizing background information during research; assisting first drafts; assisting translation and polish for cross-language versions.
  • Where AI is not used: test data and ranking scores are never fabricated by AI; conclusions are never delegated to AI; nothing is published without human review.
  • Human review: every piece of content is verified by Stephen Liu before publication — facts checked, examples re-run, sources confirmed — and only then goes live. Unverified AI output is never published.

Fact-checking process

  1. Tools and tutorials: examples are run in the current public environment, with a reproducible result recorded; test environment and limitations are stated.
  2. Rankings: scoring dimensions, data sources, collection dates and update cycle are published with each ranking, so anyone can recompute the same ranking from the same public data.
  3. Citations: third-party data cited is linked to its original source; claims whose original source cannot be found do not go into the article.
  4. Commercial separation: advertising and commercial partnerships take no part in editorial review and never change tutorial conclusions or ranking results.

Correction mechanism

If you spot an error on a page (facts, data, steps, ranking calculations), email xbdyleusajxpmdg6@gmail.com with: the page address, the wrong statement or location, and what you observed to be correct.

  • I reproduce and verify the issue before changing the page;
  • every correction is marked on the page with the correction date and what was changed — never silently edited;
  • factual corrections are targeted to be handled within 7 business days.
