网站是什么

HaoWordTool（haowordtool.com）主要有两块内容：

  • 免费在线工具——浏览器里直接可用的单词求解器、词汇工具等，打开即用。
  • AI 工具增长榜单与实测——跟踪 AI 工具产品的增长数据与实际表现，发布带实测过程的对比与排名。

内容怎么做

如实披露我们的工作方式：AI 辅助起草 + 人工核验校对。选题和研究由人完成；初稿可以用 AI 辅助起草，但每一篇发布前都经过人工核验、事实校对和改写，确认无误后才上线。洗稿、自动批量发布、不经核验的 AI 内容是不会出现的（详见编辑政策）。

榜单方法公开、可复现：每一份榜单都会写明评分维度、数据来源、数据采集时间与更新周期，你可以用同样的公开数据复算出同样的排名。

网站怎么赚钱

部分页面会展示明确标注的广告。广告和任何商业合作都不会改变教程结论和榜单排名，排名只按公开方法算出。

Who I am

I am Stephen Liu, the person who runs HaoWordTool. It is an independent one-person project — no team, no company, no funding, no awards. I build and maintain it myself, day to day.

What this site is

HaoWordTool (haowordtool.com) has two main lines of content:

  • Free online tools — browser-based word solvers and vocabulary tools that work the moment you open them.
  • AI tool growth rankings and hands-on tests — tracking growth data and real-world performance of AI tool products, published as tested comparisons and rankings.

How content is made

Full disclosure of how we work: AI-assisted drafting with human verification and proofreading. Topic selection and research are done by a person; drafts may be assisted by AI, but every article is human-verified, fact-checked and rewritten before it goes live. Rewritten, auto-published, or unverified AI content does not appear here (see the editorial policy).

Ranking methodology is public and reproducible: each ranking states its scoring dimensions, data sources, collection dates and update cycle, so you can recompute the same ranking from the same public data.

How the site earns money

Some pages display clearly labelled advertising. Advertising and commercial partnerships never change tutorial conclusions or ranking results — rankings are computed by the public method only.

How to reach me

Email me at xbdyleusajxpmdg6@gmail.com about editorial questions, factual corrections or broken tools. Please include the page address and where the error is; I verify each report and mark the correction date on the page (see the correction mechanism in the editorial policy). I maintain this site alone, not as a real-time help desk, but every report about a factual error is handled.
