About us

A small word tool with an editor’s sense of responsibility.

Letter Solver Pro helps word-game players, learners, and teachers move from a jumble of letters to an explained pattern.

What we publish

We maintain a browser-based letter solver and an original reference library about anagrams, spelling, vocabulary, classroom use, crosswords, and game strategy. Pages are written around specific problems and concrete examples rather than mass-generated word lists.

Who operates the site

Letter Solver Pro is an independent project operated from Hong Kong SAR by Stephen Liu. Editorial and support questions can be sent through the details on the Contact page.

Editorial standards

We distinguish exact anagrams from subset searches, label the limits of our curated word list, avoid claiming universal game validity, and correct errors when they are reported. Articles include an updated date and focus on reusable reasoning.

Independence

This site is not affiliated with Hasbro, Mattel, The New York Times, or any word-game publisher. Product and game names belong to their respective owners. Advertising, when present, does not determine editorial conclusions.
