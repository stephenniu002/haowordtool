FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to supply my own proxies?
No. Every request is routed through our managed residential and mobile proxy pools. You only ever talk to our API — we handle rotation, fingerprinting, retries and blocking.
How fast are the endpoints?
Median response time is around 380ms. Frequently requested profiles and posts are served from cache in under 120ms. Uncached, deep-scroll requests typically complete in 1–3 seconds.
Is this legal?
ScrapeLayer only accesses publicly available data — the same content any logged-out visitor can see. We don't bypass authentication or access private accounts. That said, you are responsible for how you use the data and for complying with platform terms and applicable laws such as GDPR.
What counts as a credit?
One successful API request equals one credit, regardless of how much data it returns. Requests that fail on our side — timeouts, 5xx errors, empty results — are never charged.
Can I use it for commercial projects?
Yes. All paid plans include a commercial license. You can build and sell products, dashboards, analytics tools and internal systems on top of the API.
What happens if I run out of credits?
On Pro and Scale plans, overage is billed automatically at your plan's per-credit rate — we never cut off your traffic mid-month. On Starter, requests return a 402 until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from the dashboard in one click, no phone calls or emails required. You keep access until the end of your current billing period, and unused credits roll over for 30 days.
